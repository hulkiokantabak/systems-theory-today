## ADOPT (surfaced for ratification, concrete)

1. **Repair episodes as the pre-registered case-coding UNIT** under the thirteen-node map. Their episode (destabilize → sense → legitimate → coordinate → act → recover) is not a rival to our causal hypothesis — it is the **sampling unit our model lacks** (Turchin: model + coded episodes is exactly the cliodynamics pairing). It unblocks Q-001 with **codable clocks** (time-to-sense, time-to-legitimate, time-to-coordinate, time-to-repair). *Condition (Turchin's dissent, binding): pre-registered inclusion/boundary criteria BEFORE any episode is coded — analyst-drawn boundaries are selection-on-the-dependent-variable waiting to happen.*
2. **Lag-type decomposition on the Y-layer edges** (epistemic / capacity / coordination / political / **obstruction**). Obstruction is missing from our graph as a distinct mechanism: "the system cannot adapt" (Theory A) vs "capacity exists, an interest blocks it" (the wealth pump) are currently indistinguishable — this decomposition makes the **A-vs-D3 crux codable at the episode level**.
3. **D-007 — authority theater (reader-facing) — as a new standing governor.** We operationalized *internal* theater (the placebo IS an authority-theater detector, and it just fired on us — C-025: form fooled our own instrument). We have **no** governor for procedural pageantry persuading *readers* — acute once the repo opens 2026-10-01. C-025 is the arming evidence.
4. **Evidence-ladder, ADAPTED not adopted:** rung-label the causal-hypothesis edges and theory docs (fact / mechanism / cross-case / theory / normative) — honestly uncomfortable, which is the point — **plus the station their ladder lacks:** an orthogonal test-status tag (*untested / pre-registered / run: survived / run: falsified*). Add "silent rung-merge" as a codable catch category. Guard: a "cross-case pattern" requires stated N and provenance.
5. **Rule 17b (small, pre-emptive):** any prescriptive output carries a coercion-and-distribution note (their agency/justice safeguards have no home in our rules because we barely prescribe — the moment we do, the gap goes live).
6. **History as case base:** the HISTORY doc is a genealogy of systems *of* thought; the project has no historical *episode base* (Rome's third-century crisis, Song reform cycles, Tanzimat, Meiji — natural experiments, unused). Priority: episode base first, canon expansion second (both wanted; only the first raises the falsifiable count).
