- It **is** the author's ratifying contact — the terminal act the whole provisional tower was waiting for. Under Rule 11, the author's direct judgment governs; the entrenched R24 firing gate and the never-prune list are touched only by this act, and only where he directed.
- It **is** a completion, not a new build. Per the author's own ruling (decision 2), the moratorium is declared **reversible** — this closes *this* build; it does not forbid a future one.
- It **is not** a fourth standing delegation. Nothing here was self-administered under a blanket "finalize everything." The author made the load-bearing calls himself; Code executed them and applied a named, pre-disclosed residue he authorized.
- It **does not** manufacture a foreign vantage. The author ruled (decision 3) that no genuinely-outside reader, grader, or forker will come in this build, and that **his judgment stands as the terminal ratifier.** Every external-dependent inferential claim is therefore conceded as *conclusively untestable in this closed, one-model setup* — not falsified, not confirmed, closed.
