# CANDIDATE THEORIES

Version: 0.2 · Status: Seeded (provisional, rival, forkable) · Last updated: Session 2 *(pressure-test accounts propagated to the thirteen-test layered set)*

*Three seed theories produced by the founding seven-round deliberation. They are **not** the project's conclusions and not a consensus — they are the most defensible starting points the panel could reach, each shipped **with its own weaknesses named in its own text**, per the Round-7 resolution. They are lettered A/B/C and, once public, are never renumbered; they are meant to be forked, strengthened, broken, merged, or replaced.*

*The relationship in one line: **A is the condition, B is a mechanism operating inside that condition, C is the response to both.** They are three faces — diagnosis, mechanism, response — not three contestants for a single throne. The panel nonetheless rated their strength *as theories* (Round 7); those ratings are recorded at the end of each.*

---

## How to read a candidate theory

Each has the same anatomy, so they can be compared and so a contributor knows exactly where to push:

1. **Claim** — the theory in one sentence.
2. **Master variable / mechanism** — the thing it says to watch.
3. **Exposition** — how it works.
4. **The pressure-tests** — what it says about each, across the layered set (drivers → dynamics → symptoms + the master condition; `GOALS.md`, `panel/SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md`).
5. **Falsifiable commitments** — what would have to be true, and what would prove it wrong. *(This is the section that separates a theory from a mood. It is also the section most in need of work — see each theory's honest self-assessment.)*
6. **Lineage** — whose shoulders it stands on.
7. **Known weaknesses** — where it is soft, stated by its own authors.
8. **How to fork it** — the most valuable next moves.

# Theory A — The Adaptation Gap
### *(alt. names: Coherence Collapse · the Complexity–Comprehension Gap · the Overwhelm)*

**Claim.** The defining condition of *today* is a widening gap between the **complexity and speed of the systems humans have built** and the **adaptive and meaning-making capacity of the humans and institutions inside them.** Most of the symptoms — across the drivers, the dynamics, and the felt surface — are downstream of that gap.

**Master variable.** The *rate difference*: (rate of change of human-built systems) − (rate of adaptation of institutions, cognition, and culture). When the difference is small, societies feel coherent and governable. When it opens wide, they feel overwhelmed, incomprehensible, and out of control — the ambient experience of the 2020s.

**Exposition.** Human biology, institutions, and shared meaning-systems all update *slowly*. Genes update over millennia; deep institutions (law, education, governance) over generations; cultural common sense over decades. Technology and the coupling it creates now update over *months*. E.O. Wilson's compression is the folk version: *we have paleolithic emotions, medieval institutions, and god-like technology.* The three were once roughly matched to their environment; they no longer are, and the mismatch is growing.

The gap is not merely cognitive overload in individuals. It is **structural**: our institutions were designed for a slower, more local, more legible world, and they now govern a faster, denser, more opaque one. A parliament legislating on a technology that is obsolete before the bill passes is the gap made visible. A person whose evolved threat-detection is fed a global, algorithmically-curated firehose of threats is the gap made visible. A culture whose shared references fragment faster than new shared references can form is the gap made visible.

The gap explains *why now* without appealing to a change in human nature or a villain: nothing about humans got worse; the *distance between our tools and our capacity to absorb them* crossed a threshold. That threshold-crossing is what the Glossary means by "today."

**The pressure-tests (the layered set).** Theory A speaks first to **Layer 1 — Drivers**, because it *is* a driver theory: **D1 Acceleration** is its own master variable (the rate-gap), and it now treats **D4 Machine intelligence** as the newest and fastest accelerant widening that gap. It reads **Layer 2 — Dynamics** as the gap surfacing in different subsystems: **Y1 epistemic breakdown** (the capacity to *check* falling behind the capacity to *fabricate*), **Y2 coordination failure** (shared understanding fragmenting faster than coupled systems can be governed — the gap *is* a coordination gap), **Y3 institutional & legitimacy decay** (institutions built for a slower, more legible world losing capacity and trust — Ibn Khaldun and Turchin, on their own clocks), and **Y4 ecological overshoot** (a planetary system with long delays driven past limits by an economy on short horizons — the canonical gap, "overshoot" in one word). The **Layer 3 — Symptoms** are the felt residue: **S1 fertility collapse** (childbearing out-competed by a life built for other optimizations, while the institutions that once made children the default thin faster than replacements form), **S3 populism** (a demand for the *restoration of legibility and agency* when the world becomes incomprehensible — Fromm's flight from an unbearable freedom), and **S4 anomie/loneliness** (the inner face of a gap that has outrun the shared frames people need to feel located). Where A is weak by construction: **D2 optimization dynamics** and **S2 the attention economy** are Theory B's to explain (A says *the gap opened*; B says *what rushed in*), and **D3 the wealth pump** is a driver A must *absorb* rather than derive — Turchin's measurable inequality mechanism may be a distinct, rival driver, not a special case of acceleration (see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` D-002, D-006). Beneath all of it, **M — the coherence vacuum**: A treats meaning as one more slow-updating system, which the meaning-bloc insists is the category error (below, and Q-002).

**Falsifiable commitments.** *(The theory's honest weak point — see below.)* Candidate operationalizations queued for Phase 1:
- Construct a **rate-of-technological-change** index and a **rate-of-institutional-adaptation** index (e.g. time-to-regulate a new technology; time-to-update core curricula; legislative cycle time vs. product cycle time). The theory predicts the **divergence** of these two series tracks measurable proxies of "overwhelm" (declining institutional trust, rising anomie, rising demand for simplifying politics).
- Prediction: societies or sectors where the two rates are **closer** (slower tech adoption *or* faster institutional adaptation) should show *milder* versions of the symptoms, all else equal.
- **What would falsify it:** if the symptoms are as severe, or worse, where the rate-gap is *narrow*; if the symptoms correlate better with a specific driver (inequality alone; a specific technology; a specific policy regime) than with the composite gap; if the gap can be shown to be an artifact of the measures rather than a real quantity.

**Lineage.** E.O. Wilson (the mismatch compression); Meadows (overshoot, limits, delay); Alvin Toffler (*Future Shock* — "too much change in too short a time"); Ibn Khaldun and Turchin (institutions and cohesion decaying on their own clocks); the general evolutionary-mismatch literature. Aristotle's late endorsement in Round 3: "our tools outran our understanding" is the most Aristotelian diagnosis available.

**Known weaknesses (stated by its authors).**
- **Falsifiability.** Turchin's standing objection: "the gap" is intuitive but hard to *measure*, and an unmeasurable master variable is a story, not a science. Until the two rate-indices exist and behave as predicted, Theory A is a compelling frame, not a tested theory. This is its single greatest liability and its top Phase-1 task.
- **Near-tautology risk.** "Things are hard because change is fast" can be made to explain anything, which means it risks explaining nothing. The defense is the specific, signed, measurable rate-gap — without it, the theory slides toward vacuity.
- **The meaning objection.** Nietzsche/Heidegger/Le Guin: even a perfectly measured gap describes a *mismatch* and says nothing about the *meaning-wound* underneath — why the loss of a shared "why" is not just another lagging institution but the ground the whole thing stands on. Theory A treats meaning as one more slow-updating system; the meaning-bloc insists that is the category error.

**How to fork it.** Build the two rate-indices and test the divergence claim (highest value). Or attack it: find a symptom that is *worse* where the gap is narrow. Or split it: separate the *cognitive* gap (individuals overwhelmed) from the *institutional* gap (organizations too slow) and test whether they move together or apart.

**Round-7 strength rating (as a theory):** median **7/10** (Meadows 9 / Turchin 4). Best integrative reach; weakest falsifiability.

# Theory B — The Optimization Ecology
### *(alt. name: Autopoietic Capture)*

**Claim.** Markets, media, and technical systems have become **self-reproducing optimizers** that capture human drives — attention, dopamine, outrage, status, desire — as *fuel*, and increasingly run **without human ends**, optimizing for their own persistence and growth. The human is becoming an **input** to systems that no longer serve human purposes, and were never designed by anyone to.

**Mechanism.** *Selection without a designer.* Three ingredients:
1. **Autopoiesis** (Luhmann, from Maturana/Varela): modern subsystems — the economy, media, technology — are self-referential. They reproduce themselves according to their own internal code (money makes money; engagement makes engagement; capability makes capability), not according to any external human goal.
2. **Capture:** these systems run on human drives as their energy source. The attention economy literally converts human attention into revenue; markets convert desire into demand; outrage-media convert the threat-response into engagement.
3. **Darwinian selection:** among competing products, media, and firms, the ones that capture human drives *most effectively* survive and spread. No designer needs to intend the outcome. A feed that better hijacks attention out-competes one that doesn't; over many cycles, the ecology fills with maximally-capturing systems — **as if** designed to exploit us, because selection did the designing.

The crucial and easily-missed point: **there is no cabal.** The outcome looks intentional — everything seems engineered to addict, enrage, and extract — but it is the emergent product of optimization under selection, not a plan. Treating it as a conspiracy both overstates human control and misdiagnoses the cure. This is *the disease of a system optimizing for the wrong thing at scale*, and the "wrong thing" was selected for, not chosen.

**Exposition.** Where Theory A says *the gap opened*, Theory B says *here is what rushed in to fill it.* Into the space between human capacity and system complexity, self-optimizing systems moved, and they optimize for **their own reproduction** using **us** as substrate. Zuboff's surveillance capitalism (human experience as free raw material for prediction products), Han's achievement-subject (who exploits *himself* and calls it freedom), and Luhmann's functionally-differentiated society (subsystems that cannot be steered from outside) are three views of the same animal. Heidegger's *Gestell* — the epoch in which everything shows up as optimizable standing-reserve — is its philosophical name; Theory B is an attempt to make the *Gestell* mechanistic and measurable.

**The pressure-tests (the layered set).** Theory B is a **Layer 1 — Driver** theory centered on **D2 optimization dynamics**, and it now explicitly claims **D4 machine intelligence** as the escalation that hands the optimizers their sharpest tools (ubiquitous sensors, machine learning, planetary distribution) — D4 is *why the mechanism reached full resolution now*. It owns the surface symptom **S2 the attention/dopamine economy** as its home case (engagement-optimizers evolved by A/B testing to deliver supernormal stimuli; Lembke at the individual scale, the optimization ecology at the system scale), and reads **Y1 epistemic breakdown** as a *selection environment* that rewards unreality structurally — which is why "post-truth" undershoots: it frames as a *culture of lying* what is really a reward landscape. **Y2 coordination failure** is Luhmann's hard result (functionally-differentiated subsystems cannot steer one another; coordination fails from *structure*, not stupidity), and **Y4 ecological overshoot** is what an economy autopoietically optimizing for growth does to a finite planet it cannot, from inside its own code, price. On the symptoms it shares with A: **S1 fertility** (a *captured* choice, shaped by a system optimizing for labor-force participation and consumption) and **S3 populism** (a revolt of the captured, which the same attention-optimizers then capture and amplify because insurgent rage is maximally engaging). B connects naturally to **D3 the wealth pump** — capital as a self-valorizing optimizer, Marx's "value in motion," is the same animal seen economically — and to **S4 anomie** (Han's achievement-subject, who exploits himself and calls it freedom). Its relation to **M — the coherence vacuum**: B explains how the optimizers *hollow out* shared meaning as a side-effect of capturing its substrate, without claiming to restore it. (**D1 acceleration** is Theory A's; B is what rushes into the space A's gap opens.)

**Falsifiable commitments.**
- Prediction: in any attention market, **measured engagement-optimization** (recommender aggressiveness, A/B-tested for time-on-platform) should correlate with **measured** rises in the pathologies the theory names (compulsive-use metrics, affective polarization, spread-rate of false vs. true claims). Engagement-optimization is *already measurable*, which gives B a head start on A.
- Prediction: systems explicitly **re-optimized** for a human end (e.g. platforms that optimize for user-reported time-well-spent rather than raw engagement) should show *reduced* pathology — a natural experiment already partly running.
- **What would falsify it:** if pathologies rise *independent* of engagement-optimization; if de-optimized systems show no improvement; if the outcomes track a designer's *intent* better than a selection *pressure* (which would make it a conspiracy theory after all, and a different — weaker — claim).

**Lineage.** Luhmann (autopoiesis, functional differentiation); Zuboff (surveillance capitalism, instrumentarian power); Han (psychopolitics, the burnout/achievement subject); Marx (capital as a self-valorizing subject — "value in motion"); Darwin/market-selection; Tinbergen (supernormal stimulus); Heidegger (*Gestell*) as the philosophical frame; Lembke (the neurochemical mechanism).

**Known weaknesses (stated by its authors).**
- **Conspiracy-shape.** Kant's standing objection: a theory in which everything is secretly optimized to exploit us is *narratively* close to a paranoid worldview, and the human mind loves a villain. The theory's integrity depends entirely on holding the line — **selection, not a cabal** — and that line is psychologically hard to hold and easy for readers to lose. If it slides into "they designed it to addict us," it becomes false and dangerous.
- **Agency erasure.** If humans are "just inputs," the theory risks denying the real agency and resistance that plainly exist (people do log off; systems do get re-designed; regulation does bite). An over-strong version is both empirically wrong and politically demobilizing.
- **Functionalism.** Explaining every feature by "it was selected because it captures" can become unfalsifiable in the same way A can — any outcome can be back-rationalized as adaptive. The defense is, again, the specific measured pressure, not the story.

**How to fork it.** Run the natural experiment: compare engagement-optimized vs. deliberately de-optimized systems on measured pathology (highest value, and partly available now). Or stress the boundary between B and a conspiracy theory: specify precisely what evidence distinguishes "selected to capture" from "designed to capture," since the theory lives or dies on that distinction.

**Round-7 strength rating (as a theory):** median **7/10** (Zuboff 9 / Kant 5). Best mechanism; greatest risk of conspiracy-shaped over-reach.

# Theory C — Distributed Coherence
### *(alt. name: The Commons of Sense-Making)*

**Claim.** Because **no single mind can hold the whole** — this is now a structural fact, not a failure of nerve — a systems theory adequate to today cannot take the form of a monograph authored by one thinker. It must be **distributed, living, plural, and computationally-augmented**: a *commons of sense-making* rather than a doctrine. The response to a world too complex for any one vantage is not a better vantage but a better *way of holding many vantages together.*

**Mechanism / form.** Coherence achieved not by *fusion in one head* but by *structured composition across many* — models, communities, disciplines, and tools — governed so the whole stays honest, plural, and self-correcting. Ostrom's core finding is the load-bearing beam: **commons can be governed sustainably without either privatization or a central authority**, given the right rules (clear boundaries, graduated participation, collective revision, nested governance). Applied to knowledge: a shared theory can be maintained by a community without a single author-owner and without collapsing into noise — *if* it is governed like a well-run commons.

**Exposition.** Theories A and B diagnose. Theory C responds — and it responds *reflexively*, because it is a claim about **what form a theory must take**, and the only honest way to make that claim is to *be* an instance of it. Hence this project. The old total system failed for reasons the History document lays out (Gödel, specialization, pace, the meaning-vacuum, computational irreducibility); those failures are not going away, so the response cannot be "try harder to write the book." The response is to change the *medium*: from a finished artifact produced by one mind to a **living process** maintained by many, in which:
- **Disagreement is preserved, not resolved** (designed disagreement; dissent as a first-class output).
- **The whole is held distributively** — no participant holds all of it; the *commons* holds it, the way a market "knows" a price no trader knows, or an ecosystem "computes" a balance no organism intends.
- **Computation augments** but does not replace the human deliberation (models make the couplings explicit and runnable; the panel and the community decide what they mean).
- **Everything is provisional** — a metamodern "proto-synthesis" (Freinacht, Josephson Storm): totality attempted but held open, reconstruction *after* deconstruction, never a closed system.

**The medium is the thesis.** Theory C's strongest evidence is not an argument but a demonstration: whether *this repository* — its panel, its preserved dissents, its catches-and-learnings loop, its forkability — actually catches things a lone author would miss (Goal S5). If it does, C is being proven. If it produces only the illusion of rigor while a single chair quietly makes all the real decisions, C is being falsified in real time — which is exactly why L-001 (the chair's resolution-bias) is the project's most-watched risk.

**The pressure-tests (the layered set).** C does not compete with A and B to explain the symptoms; it explains **why we failed to understand them until now, and how we might start to.** Each test is transdisciplinary (fertility is economic + cultural + biological + technological; ecological overshoot is physical + political + economic + psychological), so each *exceeds* any single discipline's grasp — which is precisely why no single vantage has explained them, and why a composed, plural, computational commons is the instrument the problems actually require. The Session-2 move itself — typing the thirteen into coupled layers with directed cross-arrows, held as a *contested* hypothesis rather than a settled taxonomy — is an instance of C's claim: the object adequate to whole-system problems is a many-vantage, revisable structure, not a single author's list. C's account of the thirteen is meta: *they are hard because they are whole-system problems met with fragmented tools; the cure is a tool shaped like the problems.*

**Falsifiable commitments.**
- Prediction (the project as experiment): a **panel of designed disagreement + a preserved catches/learnings loop** will surface a measurably higher rate of *caught errors and retained live objections* than a single-author process on the same questions. (Session 1 produced six catches, three of them the chair catching its own bias — a first data point.)
- Prediction: a **forkable, commons-governed** theory will attract genuine rival contributions and improve along tracked metrics (falsifiable claims produced, pressure-tests operationalized) faster than a closed one.
- **What would falsify it:** if the "commons" produces only volume without improved error-catching or rising rigor; if all real decisions collapse onto one node (the chair) so the plurality is cosmetic; if forking produces fragmentation without any mechanism of re-integration, so coherence is *lost* rather than distributed. C claims *distributed coherence*; mere distribution with no coherence would refute it.

**Lineage.** Ostrom (governing the commons); Hayek's price-system and the broader "distributed knowledge" tradition (a system computing what no participant knows) — used critically, not as ideology; complexity science and collective intelligence; metamodernism (Freinacht, Vermeulen/van den Akker, Josephson Storm — the provisional proto-synthesis); Turchin's cliodynamics (the proof that *computational, collaborative, data-driven* social theory is possible at all); and the project's own method (designed disagreement). Le Guin sits at its heart and its edge simultaneously: a commons of sense-making is a Taoist, non-mastering way to hold a system — *and* she is the one who warns that if it becomes a machine for mastery it has joined the disease.

**Known weaknesses (stated by its authors).**
- **It may evade the wound.** Nietzsche's sharpest cut (he rated it lowest, 3/10): *a method is not a meaning.* C describes a superior *procedure* for producing understanding and says nothing about the shared *why* whose loss is the master puzzle. A perfectly-governed commons that explains everything and means nothing has not touched the thing Nietzsche keeps pointing at. C's partial answer — that *sense-making together* is itself meaning-bearing, not merely structural — is a hope, not yet a demonstration.
- **Self-serving reflexivity.** C is the theory that says "a project exactly like this one is the answer," which is suspiciously convenient and must be held to a *higher* evidentiary bar precisely because the project has an interest in its truth. Luhmann's related warning: beware the system (this project) optimizing for its own continuation and mistaking that for success — the very pathology Theory B describes, now a risk *inside* the cure.
- **Distribution vs. coherence.** The hard part is the second word. Commons can also produce noise, capture, and fragmentation (the failure modes of the actual internet). C asserts that governance can hold coherence across distribution; that assertion is the whole bet and is not yet won.

**How to fork it.** The most valuable fork is *adversarial participation*: contribute a genuine rival to A or B and see whether the commons integrates or merely accumulates it. Or attack the reflexive claim directly: design the metric that would show the plurality is cosmetic (all decisions collapsing onto the chair), and run it against this repository. Or take up Nietzsche's challenge: show whether a commons of sense-making can generate *shared meaning*, or only shared description.

**Round-7 strength rating (as a theory):** median **6/10** (Ostrom 9 / Nietzsche 3). Best practice and reflexivity; most exposed to the meaning-objection and to self-serving convenience.

## The three together

| | **A — Adaptation Gap** | **B — Optimization Ecology** | **C — Distributed Coherence** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kind** | Diagnosis (the condition) | Mechanism (what fills it) | Response (how to think/act) |
| **One line** | Systems outran our capacity | Self-optimizers capture us as fuel | Hold the whole as a plural commons |
| **Master term** | The rate-gap | Selection without a designer | Governed distributed sense-making |
| **Strongest at** | Integrative reach | Concrete mechanism | Reflexivity, practice |
| **Weakest at** | Falsifiability | Conspiracy-shape risk | The meaning-wound |
| **Top fork** | Build the two rate-indices | Run the de-optimization experiment | Contribute a real rival, test integration |
| **R7 strength** | 7/10 | 7/10 | 6/10 |

**Why ship all three.** They are not redundant and not truly rival: A tells you *what condition you are in*, B tells you *what is exploiting that condition*, C tells you *how to build understanding of both without pretending one mind can*. Ranking them is less useful than *composing* them — and composing rival partial theories into a provisional whole, with the seams and dissents left visible, is itself the demonstration of Theory C. **The three theories are, together, an instance of the third one.**

## Standing dissents against the whole slate (preserved from Round 7)
1. **Heidegger:** all three deepen the enframing; the honest act is to keep the doubt alive, not to seed theories.
2. **Nietzsche:** all three route around the meaning-wound, which is the ground of the problems; a computed map cannot restore a shared *why*.
3. **Turchin & Marx:** A and (weakly) B trade testability for a satisfying story; prefer measurable structural drivers to master-variables.
4. **Kant:** all three are systems of appearances; guard permanently against mistaking the map for the territory.
5. **Luhmann:** the honest form is a theory *of differentiation*, not a unified doctrine; and beware C's project optimizing for its own survival.
6. **Le Guin:** if any of these becomes a machine for mastering rather than a tool for living, it has become the disease it set out to diagnose.

*These six dissents are not appendices to the theories. They are their **governors** — the conditions under which each theory is allowed to keep operating. A fork that removes them is not a stronger theory; it is a more dangerous one.*
