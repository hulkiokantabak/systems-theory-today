DRAFT INCLUSION AND BOUNDARY CRITERIA — for a historical case-coding study (under external review before freeze)

You are reviewing a research team's DRAFT criteria for what counts as one codable case ("episode") in a comparative historical study. The criteria are not yet frozen; your review happens before any case is coded. Read everything below, then follow INSTRUCTIONS.md.

# 1. The unit

An "episode" is a bounded historical or contemporary passage in which a system is destabilized and a repair is attempted: a passage through six stages — destabilize -> sense -> legitimate -> coordinate -> act -> recover — with the last stage explicitly allowed to be absent (censoring, section 2 rule 4). The unit is the episode, not the polity: one polity may hold many episodes; one episode may span institutions.

Four clocks are read on each episode (the initial instrument set; any additional clock requires its own pre-registration before first use):

| Clock | Start event (operational) | Stop event (operational) |
|---|---|---|
| time-to-sense | first dated documentary evidence of the destabilization's onset (the event itself, not later narration) | first dated evidence that an actor with any standing correctly names the destabilization as requiring response |
| time-to-legitimate | the sense stop-event | first dated evidence that a response-authority is accepted by the actors whose cooperation the response requires (mandate, decree, coalition, emergency powers) |
| time-to-coordinate | the legitimate stop-event | first dated evidence of jointly-committed action across the units whose joint action the response requires |
| time-to-repair | the destabilization onset | first dated evidence the destabilizing pressure is absorbed, routed around, or dissolved — or censoring (rule 4) |

No clock-denominated claim is reported below a pre-registered inter-coder reliability floor on clock readings; clock readings from unfrozen criteria are void.

# 2. Boundary and inclusion criteria (DRAFT — the freeze is a later, separate act)

1. INCLUSION: a candidate episode enters the base only if (a) a dated destabilization event is documentable from admissible sources (rule 3); (b) at least two of the six stages are independently documentable; (c) the episode's boundaries — onset and close/censor — can be stated so that a coder outside the drafting team could apply them from a dossier alone, without any of the team's context.
2. EXCLUSION (stated with the same precision): NOT an episode: gradual secular drift with no dateable destabilization; a pure policy proposal never legitimated or acted on; a crisis narrative whose only source is retrospective synthesis (no contemporaneous documentary layer); an episode whose boundaries can only be drawn by outcome-knowledge (boundary-by-hindsight is selection on the dependent variable).
3. THE CASE-UNIVERSE (declared sampling frame): the population from which episodes are drawn is declared before coding: polities/systems with a surviving contemporaneous documentary record, drawn from the admissible source classes — published documentary editions, court/administrative records, dated official acts, contemporaneous correspondence/chronicles with established provenance, and (for contemporary episodes) the dated public record under a two-dossier ex-ante/outcome separation discipline. Episodes may not be nominated from outside the stated frame.
4. RIGHT-CENSORING (binding): an episode that never reaches "recover" is data, not a non-case — coded with time-to-repair censored at the record's end. Failed repairs must be codable, or the base is survivorship-biased at birth.
5. REPAIR-FOR-WHOM (binding): every episode coding carries a "repair-for-whom" field — whose order was "recovered," and who paid for the recovery. "Repaired" is a valuation; the coder signs it.
6. VOID-PLUS-CATCH: any episode coded before the criteria are frozen is void and logged as an error.

# 3. What this unit cannot see (a design fork, open)

The destabilize-first grammar requires a destabilization event to open an episode — so it is structurally blind to maintenance-shaped repair: care systems, mutual aid, prevention that succeeds, where the repair consists of the crisis never arriving. The open fork (to be cut by the study's principal, not by coders):

- Option (i) — a maintenance-episode variant: a second opener — documented near-miss or sustained-load evidence (the flood that did not breach because the levee was maintained; the epidemic curve that stayed flat) — with its own clock semantics defined before first use.
- Option (ii) — the exclusion recorded as a standing, dated limitation.

Until the fork is cut, no maintenance-shaped candidate is coded and the blindness stands named.
