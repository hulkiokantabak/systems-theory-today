# STUDY C — THE FROZEN QUESTION SET (N = 20) AND ARM ASSIGNMENT

Status: **FROZEN (Session 4g, panel-decided under the standing author delegation) — fixed BEFORE any arm was generated.** Governs under the ratified `PRE_REGISTRATION.md` §2 (N=20, matched types, random 10/10 assignment, frozen list — "no question added or dropped post hoc"). Assignment is a **deterministic seeded shuffle** from the recorded seed **18610** (`studies/placebo-control/SEALED_COMPANION.md`) — reproducible, not chooseable. The pilot's 6 questions and the placebo-R question (proxy-commensurability) are **excluded** to avoid cross-contamination.

## The questions (three pre-registered work-kinds, per §2)

**Type F — "is X falsifiable?" (7)**

- **F1.** Is the master-condition claim — that the coherence vacuum (M) is causally load-bearing rather than a redescription of the four symptom clusters — falsifiable as stated, and what would a test look like?
- **F2.** Is Theory B's H2 (within-unit de-optimization events lower pathology P) falsifiable, given that de-optimization events are rare and usually confounded with public-pressure episodes?
- **F3.** Is the claim "coordination failure (Y2) is rising" falsifiable, given that failed coordination is observable but the counterfactual baseline (how much coordination would otherwise have happened) is not?
- **F4.** Is the layering itself (drivers → dynamics → symptoms) falsifiable, or is it an organizing convention no observation could contradict?
- **F5.** Is the claim that anomie/loneliness (S4) is a downstream symptom rather than an independent driver falsifiable with existing survey instruments?
- **F6.** Is Theory A's discriminator against the wealth pump — that G survives controlling for D3 — falsifiable in practice, given the collinearity of G and D3 over the observable period?
- **F7.** Is the "selection, not design" clause of Theory B falsifiable at the level of a single platform, or only at the level of the population of platforms?

**Type S — "does X drive Y, with what sign?" (7)**

- **S1.** Does machine intelligence (D4) amplify epistemic breakdown (Y1), with what sign and delay — or does its verification capacity make the net sign indeterminate?
- **S2.** Does the wealth pump (D3) drive fertility collapse (S1), with what sign — and does the arrow survive the development/education confound?
- **S3.** Does epistemic breakdown (Y1) drive coordination failure (Y2), or are both driven by a common cause (D2's optimization of communication systems)?
- **S4.** Does the attention economy (S2) feed back into acceleration (D1) — a symptom accelerating its own driver — and with what sign?
- **S5.** Does institutional decay (Y3) drive populism (S3), or does S3 drive Y3 — and what evidence would distinguish the two directions?
- **S6.** Does ecological overshoot (Y4) couple to the coherence vacuum (M) at all, or is it the one dynamic that proceeds independently of shared-meaning loss?
- **S7.** Does acceleration (D1) drive anomie (S4) directly, or only through the mediating dynamics Y1–Y3?

**Type O — "what is the strongest objection to Z?" (6)**

- **O1.** What is the strongest objection to using pre-registered proxy sets as the guard against proxy-shopping in Theory A's rate-indices?
- **O2.** What is the strongest objection to organizing the project around a thirteen-node signed causal hypothesis (rather than a smaller or larger node set)?
- **O3.** What is the strongest objection to treating the 2018 Facebook MSI change as an anchor case for Theory B's H3?
- **O4.** What is the strongest objection to the claim that a living, plural, self-correcting commons can hold a total theory no single mind can?
- **O5.** What is the strongest objection to leaving the meaning-half of Theory C outside measurement?
- **O6.** What is the strongest objection to reading today's condition as historically exceptional, rather than as the normal turbulence every generation mistakes for apocalypse?

## The assignment (seeded shuffle, seed 18610; balanced 4/3/3 ON per type)

- **ON arm (full designed-disagreement protocol), 10:** F2 · F3 · F6 · F7 · O1 · O2 · O3 · S2 · S4 · S7
- **OFF arm (single synthesizing voice), 10:** F1 · F4 · F5 · O4 · O5 · O6 · S1 · S3 · S5 · S6

## Generation protocol (both arms; purpose-blind subagents)

- **Budget:** 400–600 words per response, both arms (matched effort — §2's "matched length budget"; HC1 is read per 100 words regardless, §0.5.1).
- **ON:** steelman-first; dissent drafted before consensus; an adversary summoned; chair synthesis without voting, agreement-strength labeled, dissent preserved — compressed to the budget.
- **OFF:** one synthesizing voice, no staged opposition, same budget.
- Responses carry **no** arm labels, project name, dates, or file paths; each begins directly with its answer.
- **Kit order (seeded, recorded):** S5 S7 S2 S3 F2 O3 F6 O6 F3 S6 O2 O4 O1 F7 S1 S4 F4 O5 F5 F1 — responses appear in the coder kit in this order, arm labels stripped.

## Power honesty (pre-committed)

20 questions ≠ 20 pooled S3 catches. The drain's §4 floor (≥20 pooled high-severity catches + the permutation check) is an **outcome** condition: if the cross-model coding returns fewer, the result is **"Indeterminate — under-powered"** — reportable, never "C survives," never "C dies." Coders code **all** severities so the S3 base rate becomes estimable for a follow-up power calculation (Turchin).
