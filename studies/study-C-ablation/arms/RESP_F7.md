**Round 1 — steelmen.**

**Turchin (strongest YES — single platform suffices).** The clause, as operationalized in H3, is a claim about *which documented signal the outcome tracks* when intent and pressure diverge. That divergence can occur inside one platform: a team ships a stated human-centered objective (memos, OKRs, public commitments) while the market rewards engagement. If the platform's trajectory follows the stated plan and defies the pressure — outcomes match the memo, not the revenue gradient — the clause is falsified *there*, in one case. One clean documented counter-case is exactly what Popperian falsification requires; you do not need a population to observe a plan beating a pressure. The bounding condition helps: a single successful, durable de-optimization event (P falls and stays down against the market's pull, by intent) is a within-unit interrupted time series. The unit of falsification is the *divergence episode*, and platforms supply episodes.

**Luhmann (strongest NO — population only).** "Selection" is constitutively a population-level predicate. Selection explains why the *surviving ensemble* looks captured: variants that capture drives outgrow variants that do not. A single platform underdetermines the mechanism — any one firm's drift toward capture is equally consistent with covert intent (the memo lied), incompetence, or founder effects; any one firm's successful resistance is consistent with slack, subsidy, or monopoly rents shielding it from the pressure. Within one unit, intent and pressure are almost never cleanly observable and independently measured: stated plans are performative documents. Only across the population — do de-optimizers systematically shrink, get outcompeted, or revert? — does "selected, not designed" acquire empirical content. F7's honest answer: single-platform tests probe *design*; only population tests probe *selection*.

**Round 2 — positions, dissent drafted first.**

**Dissent (Le Guin, drafted before any consensus).** Both positions assume the archive is readable. Stated intent is discoverable only through leaks, lawsuits, and self-serving PR; the case-selection rule will be colonized by what got leaked. Whatever level you test at, H3 may be falsifiable in principle and unfalsifiable in archival practice — record this as the standing caveat, not a footnote.

**Meadows.** Split the clause. Its *negative* half ("not design") is single-platform falsifiable: one platform whose outcome demonstrably tracks a plan against pressure refutes it for that case and, repeated, collapses B toward conspiracy (falsification condition 3). Its *positive* half ("selection") needs differential survival — population only.

**Round 3 — adversary against the emerging split-clause agreement.**

**Ibn Khaldun.** Your tidy split fails because there is no population in the relevant sense: five or six giant platforms, non-independent, imitating each other, co-evolving with the same ad market. That is a *lineage*, not a sample. "Population-level" tests will have n≈6 with correlated errors — statistically decorative. Conversely, your "single-platform" test smuggles the population back in: you cannot call a pressure "selection pressure" without knowing what happened to those who defied it. Neither level is clean; the real test is the *comparative episode set* — many intent/pressure divergences pooled across platforms and time, which is neither one platform nor a true population.

**Chair synthesis (non-voting).** Agreement-strength: **majority, not consensus.** Majority position: the clause decomposes — "not design" is falsifiable per-episode within a single platform (H3, condition 3, the de-optimization bound); "selection" proper requires cross-unit differential-survival evidence, best approximated by Ibn Khaldun's pooled episode set given the tiny, entangled platform population. Luhmann maintains partial dissent (single-unit results never license the word "selection"); Le Guin's archival-readability caveat is preserved as binding on both levels. The pre-registration should therefore label H3 verdicts *per-episode*, and reserve the full clause for the pooled analysis.
