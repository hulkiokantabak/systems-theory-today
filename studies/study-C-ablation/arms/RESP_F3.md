**Round 1 — Steelmen.**

*Popper-YES (falsifiability defended):* "Y2 is rising" is falsifiable if operationalized as a claim about coordination *performance under comparable demand*, not about an unobservable counterfactual. Specify indicators in advance — treaty ratification lag, legislative throughput per seat-share, standards-body cycle time, pandemic-response synchrony, cross-bloc scientific collaboration rates — and the claim predicts a directional trend. If the indicators improve or stay flat, the claim fails. Sciences routinely handle missing counterfactuals with cohort comparisons: we never observe the coordination that "would have happened," but we observe *relative* performance across periods facing structurally similar problems. The Montreal Protocol versus successive climate COPs is exactly such a matched pair: comparable problem class (global atmospheric commons), divergent outcomes. That contrast is evidence, and its inversion would be disconfirmation.

*Duhem-NO (the baseline objection):* Every indicator smuggles in the counterfactual. "Treaty lag is up" falsifies nothing unless you know how hard today's treaties are — and problem difficulty is endogenous to the theory itself: D1 (acceleration) says problems now outrun institutional response times, D4 changes the actor set, Y1 corrodes the shared facts coordination presupposes. So the denominator (coordination demanded) is generated by the same model whose numerator (coordination delivered) we are testing. Any failed prediction can be absorbed by adjusting the demand estimate: "coordination didn't fall; problems got harder." A claim with a freely tunable baseline is unfalsifiable in practice, whatever its logical form. Montreal-vs-COP fails as a matched pair — ozone had few producers, cheap substitutes, and no fossil-scale incumbents.

**Round 2 — Positions, dissent first.**

*DISSENT (Duhem, drafted before any consensus):* Unless the theory ships a demand model that is fixed *independently* of Y2 — with difficulty scored ex ante by parties blind to the outcome — "Y2 is rising" remains a degenerating research programme's protective belt, not a testable claim. I predict the panel will paper over this with "operationalization." Record that I reject partial fixes.

*Popper:* Falsifiable, conditional on pre-registered indicators plus explicit difficulty covariates. The claim becomes: *controlling for stated difficulty measures, coordination output declines.*

**Round 3 — Sharper adversary.**

*Lakatos (against the emerging agreement):* Both of you are asking the wrong question. Naive falsifiability is the wrong criterion for a systems claim embedded in a thirteen-node causal web; no single node-claim faces the tribunal alone. The real test is *progressive versus degenerating*: does Y2 generate novel, risky predictions the rival theories (A's adaptation gap, C's distributed coherence) do not? If Y2-rising only ever retrodicts known failures, it is degenerating even if some indicator basket is technically refutable. Conversely, if it predicted, say, that coordination fails *fastest* precisely where D2-optimized platforms mediate the actors — a prediction A and B would not both make — a confirmed novel fact matters more than the baseline problem. Demand *differential* predictions, not clean counterfactuals.

**Chair synthesis (non-voting).** Agreement-strength: **majority, not consensus.** Majority position (Popper + Lakatos, differently reasoned): the claim is falsifiable *as reconstructed* — pre-registered indicator baskets, ex-ante difficulty scoring by outcome-blind raters, and at least one risky differential prediction distinguishing Y2 from theories A and C. As commonly asserted — a bare trend claim with a floating baseline — it is not. Preserved dissent (Duhem): the difficulty covariates are themselves theory-laden via D1/Y1 endogeneity, so the reconstruction relocates rather than removes the immunizing move; he holds the claim unfalsifiable in practice until the demand model is externally anchored. The chair notes the dissent is itself testable: check whether difficulty scores drift after disconfirming results.
