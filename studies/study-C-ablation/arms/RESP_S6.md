Ecological overshoot is the strongest candidate for independence from the coherence vacuum, and the ratified graph already half-concedes this: Y4's only inbound edge is Y2 → Y4, making it the sole dynamic with no direct arrow from M and no direct arrow from any driver. Its generative machinery is biophysical — carbon stocks, drawdown rates, thermodynamic throughput — and that machinery would run under any meaning regime. A civilization with fully intact shared meaning, operating the same acceleration (D1) and optimization (D2) engines, would still be burning the same fuels at the same rates. In this narrow sense Y4 is the least meaning-dependent node in the vocabulary: nature does not read our stories, and the physics of overshoot proceeds whether or not we agree on what anything means.

But independence of generation is not independence of dynamics, and the coupling reappears in three places once the loops are traced.

First, the mediated pathway. Y2 → Y4 says overshoot happens because a world that cannot coordinate on long-horizon collective action exceeds its limits — and coordination failure is exactly where the coherence vacuum bites hardest. Shared meaning is what once made long-horizon sacrifice legible: a common frame in which "our grandchildren" named a real constituency rather than a rhetorical flourish. When M hollows out, Y2 deepens, and Y4's brake weakens. The coupling is one edge removed, but it is load-bearing.

Second, the registration problem. Overshoot only becomes a *social* fact through shared epistemics, and Y1 corrodes precisely that. The biophysical signal is unambiguous; its collective reception is not. A coherence vacuum does not cause the warming, but it fractures the interpretive commons through which warming would be converted into mandate. This matters decisively for B1, the delayed balancing loop: the graph's one hope is that acute crisis (Y4 spiking) provokes adaptive reform. B1's speed depends on whether the crisis is *commonly perceived as a crisis* — and that perception is a meaning-function. The wildfire is physical; whether it means "act together" or "the elites are lying again" is downstream of M.

Third, the return edge the graph does not yet draw. Overshoot feeds back into meaning: solastalgia, climate anxiety, the quiet reasoning of the young that a destabilized future disqualifies children (a plausible Y4 → S1 pathway alongside Y3 → S1). Overshoot is itself a meaning event — one that can either seed new coherence (a shared predicament as the germ of a shared story) or deepen the vacuum (fatalism as terminal anomie). Which way it cuts is exactly the R-loop-versus-B1 race.

The three theories converge on the same asymmetry. Theory A reads Y4 as the adaptation gap in its most literal form: G = R_c − R_a with the biosphere as the lagging adapter — no M needed to open the gap, M needed to close it. Theory B is most explicit: optimization is meaning-blind (H2's core claim), which is *why* Y4's engine runs independently of shared meaning. Theory C locates the entire remedy in coherence, distributed or otherwise.

So the answer is: asymmetric coupling. Y4 is generated almost independently of M — through the brake's absence, not the engine's presence — but it is corrected only through M. Its physics ignore meaning; its fate is decided by nothing else.
