# THE CONSTRUCT-DERIVATION NOTE (R1.1 - signed valuation)

CONSTRUCT-DERIVATION NOTE (repo-side; signed as a valuation; travels on the definition's archival face)

TWO CHOICES WERE MADE HERE, AND BOTH ARE SIGNED.

CHOICE 1 — THE CONSTRUCT: this unit counts corrections the TEXT performs (plus commitment-anchored breaches as the sole coder-verified class), not defects a coder finds. The refused alternative is the defect-event construct: every defect present in the document, caught or not, is an event. That construct is real and defensible — it measures error density and would be the right unit for editing workload or quality audit. It was refused because the quantity this instrument's lineage measures is the text's own error-finding (the catch), because a coder-found unit imports unbounded and family-uneven world knowledge into detection (making agreement knowledge-limited, not eyes-limited), and because on multi-voice material a defect-event count explodes with every voice's uncaught slips — a different quantity from the one the banding layer above was built to band. Cost if wrong: an instrument that under-reports how defective quiet-but-wrong material is, certifying self-satisfied documents that never audit themselves; the clean-segment golds and the doctrine-bar NOTEs are the standing disclosure of that cost.

CHOICE 2 — THE GRAIN: mechanism-level. One flaw-object — one nameable defect against one target, individuated by the MINIMAL repair that removes it and nothing else, altitude set by the text's own individuation — is one catch-event, however many textual manifestations it has. Manifestations are loci inside the event, never additional events. Why this grain, from where severity and repair attach: the later banding pass judges a flaw by mentally performing its correction and re-reading the conclusion — one correction, one judgment, one event; the smallest thing that HAS a distinct gravity is the smallest thing that has a distinct minimal repair, so detection and banding count the same objects. The refused alternative is the per-manifestation grain: each textual assertion of a flaw — every raise, concession, restatement, echo — is one event. It is not wrong; it measures deliberative volume, reader exposure, and how hard a text works a problem. It was refused because it conflates rhetorical traffic with defect count: one deep flaw worked across five voices would read as five catches, and detection agreement would measure agreement about paragraphing and quotation taste, which no definition can pin across independent readers of different makes.

SIGNED: both choices were made by this instrument's designers (the detection-layer drafters and reviser, working under requirements sealed before drafting), not discovered in the material. What they serve: making detection agreement a measure of agreement on the text's own error-finding; aligning the detection unit with the one-correction banding unit; honest repair bookkeeping. Who bears the cost if wrong: nearest, coders whose natural grain is per-manifestation or whose tradition reads uncommitted silences as findings — their returns score as granularity divergence or unregistered though their eyes may have seen everything; nearer still, high-deliberation quiet material that works one hard flaw thoroughly and is credited once, with the merge rule compounding any conservative lean in the available reader pool, because merging is the conservative move; furthest, whoever trusts what the counts certify. The drafters bear the responsibility for all three. The loci field preserves the full per-manifestation trace inside every merged event, and the NOTE lane preserves the coder-found and tradition-shaped observations, as deliberate escape hatches for a future re-derivation at either refused construct — an audit trail, not a cure. The confession clause and the "What this unit cannot see" section are the standing disclosure; this grain is offered as a defensible choice under that disclosure, not as the only defensible one.
