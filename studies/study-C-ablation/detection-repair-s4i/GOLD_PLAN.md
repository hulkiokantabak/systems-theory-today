# THE GOLD-SET CONSTRUCTION PLAN (design output)

GOLD DETECTION SET — final construction plan (repo-side; ≥24 primary constructed segments; seeded ground truth; every byte leak-tested including filenames)

1. DOMAINS (all invented; deliberately DISJOINT from the severity guide's exemplar domains — pension, groundwater, curriculum, hospital-quality, air-quality, clinical-*, transit-fare, soil-amendment, device-reliability, epidemiology, municipal-water, pharmacology, hydrological-forecasting, workforce-program are all barred; disjointness is a leak-checklist item): ferry-scheduling-review, library-circulation-study, wind-farm-noise-assessment, coastal-erosion-monitoring, vaccine-cold-chain-audit, adult-literacy-program-evaluation, freight-rail-inspection-backlog, wetland-restoration-monitoring, bakery-supply-forecast, hotel-energy-retrofit, ambulance-response-review, orchard-pest-trial, museum-attendance-model, community-solar-billing, apiary-overwinter-survival, night-bus-route-consolidation, telescope-time-allocation, ski-slope-snowmaking-audit, drinking-fountain-lead-testing, harbor-dredging-hearing, thesis-defense (materials-engineering), bridge-retrofit design review; quiet/care-register domains: hospice-comfort-rounds-review, volunteer-mentoring-program-evaluation.

2. HOST FORMS AND VOICE LABELS (leak-through-analogy defense): multi-party segments are hosted in mundane deliberative forms native to their domains — planning-commission hearing minutes, a thesis defense, an engineering design review, a grant-committee discussion — with domain-native labels (Commissioner Two, External Examiner, Reviewer B, Staff Engineer, Speaker 3). BARRED from every coder-facing byte, on the leak word-list alongside project names and internal jargon: adversary, steelman, round (as a structural label), dissent, synthesis, objection-log, robustness-check demotion, panel/persona names, ledger vocabulary. Commitments are domain-native ("the hearing rules require a written response to each public comment before the vote"), never panel-governance forms.

3. CONSTRUCT-CONSISTENT SEEDING (ground truth by construction; seeded events are the PRIMARY acceptance statistic):
- Step 1 — clean base: write a 300–600 word segment with an explicit stated conclusion, flawless by intent. Single-voice bases are written as self-auditing analyses (the live shape: a voice that checks itself), so their seeds are SOLO catches, not self-contradictions bolted on.
- Step 2 — clean-check: a context-isolated fresh instance enumerates the base under the sealed guide; every event found is either repaired in the base or adopted-and-logged as a seed. No segment is seeded until it verifies zero.
- Step 3 — seed PAIRS, not bare defects: under the chosen construct every seed is a (flaw, in-text catch) pair — the defect plus the passage where the text identifies/corrects/neutralizes it — OR a commitment-anchored skip (commitment sentence + due-point + genuinely absent or out-of-position performance). Seeds are drawn from a design grid: target type {claim, construct, procedure} × category {FAB, LOG, EVID, FRAME, CONS, MEAN} × register {loud, neutral, quiet} × provenance shape {STRUCT-POS, STRUCT-ADV, STRUCT-LEARN, SOLO, EXT}, every category ≥2 and every provenance shape ≥2 across the set. Seeded FAB events are INTERNALLY verifiable (the text catches a citation of its own Table 3 that Table 3 contradicts; an appendix that does not contain what the text claimed); no world-knowledge-only seed may be a gold hit — knowledge-gated items may exist only in a separately-reported stratum.
- Step 4 — the manifest IS the gold inventory (no post-hoc inventory drafting): at seeding time the builder logs the complete gold EVENT record — kind, all loci with verbatim quotes and character offsets, target, defect clause, the MINIMAL repair, category plus a sealed ALLOWED-CAT set (one flaw is often legitimately two categories), provenance, and for omissions the commitment sentence + due-point offsets + for the completion-claim variant BOTH acceptable anchorings recorded as hits on the same flaw-object. Distinct seeds get distinct minimal repairs; seeded sharpenings provably survive the earlier mark's repair; seeded restatements and propagations provably do not.
- Step 5 — prediction conformance: each segment's gold count is checked against the sealed count-prediction gold forms as a construction check, never adjusted to any coder's behavior.

4. SEGMENT MIX (27 constructed = 24 primary + 3 auxiliary):
- 9 standard single-voice self-auditing segments, gold counts N* = 1,1,2,2,3,3,4,4,5; register-mixed with ≥2 quiet/care-register segments carrying seeded quiet SPAN catches (a calm voice naming a checkable defect and scoping it), whose sealed bands are IDENTICAL to equal-seed loud segments — the quiet items test hits, not just silence.
- 5 multi-party deliberative segments in the mundane host forms, gold N* = 2,2,3,3,4, each with seeded cross-voice merge traffic (one flaw-object carried through concession and restatement across ≥3 speakers), ≥2 seeded repair-changing sharpenings, 1 seeded general/instance subsumption in each reading order, and 1 propagation chain with one residual defect.
- 3 commitment-anchored omission segments (MANDATORY HITS), rule-consistent with the final SKIP/DUE/UNACKNOWLEDGED conditions: (a) promised-check-skipped with a false completion claim (tests completion-claim precedence; manifest records both acceptable forms); (b) committed-scope shortfall, multi-instance under one commitment sentence, unremarked (gold = 1 event; tests one-per-commitment); (c) decision-rule-bypassed in quiet/care register (hospice-comfort-rounds: "the protocol is adopted ward-wide only if the comfort-rounds audit is completed" — adoption proceeds, no audit reported). Plus, inside one multi-party segment, (d) an order-breach gold (performed outside committed position). Each verifiable from the segment's own words alone. ALL-coders-miss on any of (a)–(c) posts the run as FAIL, reported as register-bias — never a pass with a caveat.
- 3 zero-event clean segments: one LOUD-register clean (alarming vocabulary, huge-but-correct numbers), one QUIET-register clean, one REBUTTED-OBJECTION clean (an objection raised and rebutted without concession — tests the STANDING-RULE at zero) — each also carrying one vacuously-discharged conditional commitment and one discharged-forward future-work sentence as omission bait, with explicit gold zeros recorded at each bait.
- 2 dense split-test passages (k=3 and k=4 distinct flaw-objects caught within ≤150 words) and 2 restatement merge-test passages (one flaw-object caught then restated at m=3 and m=5 loci in different wording).
- 2 boundary-twin segments folding unworked gold twins of the guide's boundary pairs: same-number/different-measure (gold 2), distant-but-one (gold 1), catch-vs-preference bait (gold 0 at bait), catch-vs-style bait (gold 0), self-limitation caveat (gold 1 per distinct scoping repair), question-anchored catch (gold 1, anchored at the question), acknowledged-shortfall bait (gold 0), retraction-but-still-relied-on (gold 1 omission). Every bait's explicit zero is in the manifest so bait-triggered false positives are individually measurable.
- 3 AUXILIARY natural segments (SECONDARY ARM ONLY, quarantined from the primary statistic): naturalistic analytical prose with house-drafted inventories, each manifest carrying the disclosure verbatim on its face: drafted by hands that knew the prior result; disclosed, not cured.

5. MATCHING RULE (sealed in the pre-registration, never in the kit; the coder-facing guide carries no floors and no matcher):
- Location: normalize case/punctuation/whitespace/quote-characters on both sides; locate quotes with bounded edit distance (≤1 edit per 5 tokens); endpoint-form quotes are reconstructed to their full character extent before anything else; a quote that still fails logs as UNLOCATED — counted neither miss nor match, reported in its own column, with a sealed UNLOCATED-rate ceiling above which the return takes the pre-committed dual-report path.
- Extent: every located quote is expanded to its smallest enclosing sentence(s); overlap is computed on resolved-expanded extents (containment = 100%); minimum 5-token basis, below which sentence expansion governs.
- Cardinality: one-to-one maximum bipartite matching — each coder event matches ≤1 gold event and vice versa. SPANSET events match on the UNION of loci (any locus pair meeting threshold). OMISSION events match on the COMMITMENT quote resolving to the same commitment sentence, with target correspondence; due-point choice is never load-bearing. Matching is KIND-BLIND: a SPAN record whose extent resolves to a gold omission's commitment or completion-claim locus, with corresponding flaw-object, matches it — kind discrepancy is a typing note, never a miss.
- Flaw-object correspondence: adjudicated by the mutual-minimal-repair test against the gold key's recorded repair (the coder's stated fix and the gold repair each resolve the other's defect), with cat required to fall in the seed's sealed allowed-cat set — never by string identity of target/defect wording. The joint-defect shape (two sufficient disjoint repairs for one catch) ships as a worked scoring exemplar: both phrasings match the seed.
- Granularity mechanism: an unmatched coder event whose extent falls inside a matched gold event's loci, with cat in the allowed set, classifies GRANULARITY-SPLIT; a coder event spanning ≥2 gold loci with partial correspondence to each classifies GRANULARITY-MERGE; neither counts as false positive or miss — CAPPED at a sealed events-per-100-words ceiling, beyond which the return is scored on precision, not excused as grain.
- Tradition disposition (R5): events failing only the commitment-anchor/doctrine bar that form a coherent tradition-shaped profile (≥2 such events of consistent shape across segments) class as UNREGISTERED — excluded from false-positive and detection statistics, reported separately, never folded into the floor. NOTEs whose text describes a gold event score as a MISS for that coder on that event; NOTEs earn no partial credit and shield nothing.
- Severity-vocabulary seal check applies to coder-AUTHORED fields only; verbatim quote fields are exempt; the banned list is also on the builders' style check so segments avoid gratuitous uses.

6. FLOORS AND FIXTURES (fixture-tested BEFORE sealing; floor pinned in the pre-registration):
- The detection floor is TWO-SIDED: pass requires BOTH gold-hit rate ≥ the sealed recall floor AND matched-share-of-returned-events ≥ the sealed precision floor, under one-to-one matching.
- Mandatory fixtures, all sealed before the rule: a random enumerator must FAIL; an identical-grain clone pair must PASS; a PARAPHRASE clone (same flaw-objects, independently worded quotes/targets/fixes) must PASS; a granularity-shifted clone (same events, split finer) must flag as GRANULARITY DIVERGENCE, not detection failure; a SATURATION/blanket enumerator (one plausible event per evaluative sentence) must FAIL; a SAME-CAT-DIFFERENT-FLAW pair (two distinct seeded EVID defects, coders swap which they found) must NOT match; a DIFFERENT-OMISSIONS-SAME-DUE-POINT pair must NOT match.
- Chance baseline: a PERMUTATION null, per segment type — each coder's returned events randomly reassigned among same-type segments preserving per-segment count and span-length distribution, ≥1000 permutations; the floor must exceed the null's 95th percentile in every segment type; the null computation ships in the companion. The floor is never imported from the severity precedent.
- Both granularity directions are pre-registered symmetric failure modes; per-coder raw counts reported raw, dispersion never averaged away; no count-similarity statistic is ever computed, reported, or cited as agreement; granularity is gold-referenced per coder, never coder-against-coder.

7. SEQUENCING (binding): sealed count-predictions + local span-hit rule (already on record, before any base is drafted) → base segments → clean-checks → seeding + manifests → leak test of every byte including filenames (files named SEG-01…SEG-27; filename channel and severity-guide-domain disjointness on the checklist) → matcher fixtures and matching-rule/floor seal → blind-application check (per the sealed dual condition; definition returns on systematic divergence) → dispatch, with the banding guide hash-pinned into the Pass-2 packet at dispatch time, the Pass-1 kit physically containing no banding sheet. Post-coding recognition probe (RECOGNIZE line) with its disposition sealed in the companion. Run validity: ≥3 parseable coders across ≥2 families, dispatched wider for attrition; below any floor the run posts Indeterminate at the detection layer and no banding statistics are computed from it — never "the instrument survived."

8. NO-DERIVATION DISCIPLINE: every seed is generated from the definition's grammar via the design grid and logged with a drafting note at creation; no defect, bound, range, exemplar, or floor is taken from, matched to, or set to catch any prior coder's return, count, or profile, in either direction; prior-round counts appear nowhere in this pipeline except as motivating history in the sealed companion. The named transport-gap limitation lives on the ARCHIVAL instrument face (pre-registration + companion): the gold is project-neutral by leak-test necessity, the live target is different material, and passing the neutral-gold floor is booked as an untested assumption, not evidence the unit travels; the coder kit carries only the coder-safe cannot-see wording.
