# RESPONSE 13

**Round 1 — Steelmen.**

*Advocate (YES — pre-registration is the right guard), "Fisher":* Proxy-shopping is a garden-of-forked-paths problem, and pre-registration is the only mechanism that binds the analyst *before* the data can whisper. Freezing the proxy sets for R_c and R_a, with equal default weights and alternatives demoted to robustness checks, converts G = R_c − R_a from a tunable rhetorical device into a falsifiable quantity. The falsifier is already wired in: if reasonable alternatives materially move G's trajectory, that itself flags measurement artifact. No other guard has teeth.

*Objector (NO — pre-registration is the wrong guard here), "Duhem":* Pre-registration guards against *post hoc* selection, but the fatal selection in Theory A happens *pre hoc*. R_c ("rate of change") and R_a ("rate of adaptation") are not natural kinds with a sampling frame of candidate proxies; they are open-ended constructs whose proxy universe is unenumerable. Whoever composes the frozen set has already chosen — guided by the same theoretical intuitions the study is meant to test — which indicators count as "change" and which as "adaptation." Pre-registration then *launders* that choice: it timestamps the shopping trip and calls the receipt a warrant. The guard certifies procedural innocence while leaving construct validity exactly where it was.

**Round 2 — Positions, dissent drafted first.**

*Dissent (drafted before consensus), "Neurath":* Even the robustness-check escape hatch fails, because "reasonable alternative proxy sets" is itself analyst-defined. A motivated analyst pre-registers a favorable primary set and a *conveniently narrow* alternative family; robustness across that family confirms nothing. The artifact-falsifier only bites if the alternative space is adversarially constructed, and nothing in pre-registration forces that.

*Concurrence, "Meehl":* Agree with Duhem's core: the deepest objection is **construct-selection precedes registration**. Pre-registration freezes *a* mapping from concept to measurement; it cannot certify that the mapping is the right one, or that a disconfirming mapping wasn't quietly never proposed. For latent civilizational rates, the file-drawer sits upstream of the registry.

**Round 3 — Sharper adversary against the emerging agreement.**

*Adversary, "Mayo":* You are all conceding too fast. "Every guard is incomplete" is trivially true; the question is comparative severity. Pre-registration plus the artifact-falsifier plus *published* alternative-set results is auditable by outsiders, who can propose their own proxy sets and re-run the frozen pipeline. The upstream-selection objection collapses into a demand for community-level severe testing — which pre-registration *enables* rather than blocks. The strongest honest objection is narrower: pre-registration gives **false assurance** — a compliance signal that readers mistake for construct validation — and thereby *reduces* scrutiny of the concept-to-proxy step.

**Chair synthesis (non-voting).** Agreement-strength: **majority, not consensus**. Majority position (Duhem, Meehl, Neurath, partially Mayo): the strongest objection is that pre-registration operates *downstream of the decisive degree of freedom* — the initial selection of what counts as a proxy for R_c and R_a in an unenumerable construct space — so it timestamps rather than disciplines the choice, and its compliance aura can actively suppress scrutiny of construct validity ("laundering by registry"). Preserved dissent (Fisher, Mayo): this objection under-weights the ecosystem effect — a frozen, public pipeline invites adversarial re-instrumentation by critics, which is the only severity available for latent constructs; the objection is an argument for *adversarial* proxy-set construction and third-party re-runs as a supplement, not for abandoning pre-registration. Neurath's rider is preserved as an unresolved sharpening: absent adversarially chosen alternative sets, the measurement-artifact falsifier can be gamed and G's robustness claims remain self-graded.
