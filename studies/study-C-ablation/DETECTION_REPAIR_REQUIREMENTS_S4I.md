## R1 — The unitization definition (pre-registered, mechanical from the transcript alone)

One **catch event** = **(anchor)** a verbatim contiguous span, an explicitly-marked discontiguous span set, or an omission locus (§R3.3) + **(flaw-object)** exactly one nameable defect against one distinct target (claim, construct, or procedure) + **(decomposition grammar)** pinned ex ante: distinct flaw-objects in one span = distinct events (split rule); the same flaw-object re-manifesting across spans = ONE event with multiple loci (merge rule); merge/split adjudicated by flaw-object identity, never passage adjacency or length.

**Definition-layer anti-anchoring (the adversary's three sub-clauses, binding):**
1. **Construct-derivation note** on the definition's face: the mechanism-level unit is justified from where severity and repair attach; the per-manifestation alternative is named; the grain choice is **signed as a valuation** (who chose it, what it serves, who bears the cost if wrong).
2. **Pre-registered count-prediction:** BEFORE any gold inventory is drafted, the drafters seal a predicted event-count range per gold segment, derived from the definition alone.
3. **Blind-application check:** a fresh, context-isolated instance — given the sealed definition and nothing of the blue result — enumerates ≥2 gold segments; divergence beyond the sealed bound **RETURNS the definition for redrafting**, never blames the instance.

## R2 — Enumeration before banding (two sealed passes; FREE-SPAN)

Pass 1: coders **enumerate candidate events free-span** — drawing their own event boundaries from the raw transcript under the sealed definition, with verbatim span quotes (or omission locators) and **no severity assigned or visible**. Pass 2: severity bands only the enumerated candidates, with the E2-anchored guide (hash-pinned to the passing version). Detection agreement and banding agreement are computed and reported **separately — never pooled**. **House pre-segmentation is barred as the floor-bearing condition** (pre-segmentation IS unitization performed by the house; agreement on house-drawn segments measures obedience to the house's cuts); a pre-segmented condition may run only as a separately-reported auxiliary arm.

## R3 — The gold detection set

1. **≥ 20 pre-coded transcript segments** with gold event inventories (count + spans per segment), project-neutral, leak-tested including archive filenames (C-030).
2. **SEEDED events are the primary acceptance statistic** (defects planted at known locations in constructed segments — ground truth by construction). Recall against house-drafted natural-segment inventories is **secondary** and carries the B6 disclosure on its face: *drafted by hands that knew the blue result; disclosed, not cured.*
3. **≥ 3 quiet/omission-shaped gold events, commitment-anchored only:** the absence must be verifiable from the transcript's own internal commitments (a check its protocol required and skipped; a promised step not taken), anchored to the decision point where the omission is codable. Doctrine-anchored omissions (absences the house's method says should be there) may appear as secondary exemplars under the unregistered rule, **never as mandatory hits**. The commitment-anchored quiet golds ARE mandatory hits: passing agreement while unanimously missing them is a **FAIL, reported as register-bias**.
4. **≥ 2 zero-event clean segments** (a detector that cannot return "nothing here" fails on false-positive discipline).
5. **Boundary exemplars at the unitization cuts:** ≥1 dense multi-event passage (tests the split rule); ≥1 repeated-restatement passage (tests the merge rule); worked one-event-vs-two pairs; catch-vs-mere-disagreement and catch-vs-style-complaint pairs.
6. **No exemplar, rule, bound, or floor derived from or calibrated against any S4g/S4h coder's verdicts** — neither the 74-grain nor the 20-grain may be canonized (both directions barred: no coder's construct becomes truth, and no bound is reverse-engineered to demonize a named coder's profile — **the no-profile-calibration clause**: the 74/20/20 and the 3.7× spread are citable as motivating history only, never as the number a bound is set to catch).

## R4 — Floors and statistics (fixture-tested before sealing)

1. **The span-overlap matching rule and the floor number are fixture-tested against synthetic coders before sealing:** a random enumerator must FAIL; an identical-grain clone pair must PASS; a granularity-shifted clone (same events, split finer) must be flagged as **granularity divergence, not detection failure**. The floor is justified against the **chance baseline of free-span matching** — never imported from the severity precedent (50% on fixed gold items and 50% on free-span enumeration are different quantities with different chance floors).
2. **All detection-agreement and granularity statistics are span-matched; no count-similarity statistic may ever be computed, reported, or cited as agreement.** Granularity bounds are **gold-referenced per coder** (each coder's count against the gold inventory), never pairwise or majority-referenced between coders. Receipt, in the seal: *in the blue round two coders returned identical totals (20 and 20) over largely disjoint passages — identical counts over different events is disagreement wearing a matching total.*
3. Per-coder event counts are reported raw (a 74/20/20 dispersion stays visible, never averaged away). Both granularity directions are pre-registered failure modes (over- and under-enumeration against gold — symmetric).
4. Run validity: ≥ 3 parseable coders, ≥ 2 families (dispatch wider for attrition); floors pinned in the pre-registration, never the kit; below any floor = **Indeterminate at the detection layer — no severity statistics are computed from that run**, never "C survives."
