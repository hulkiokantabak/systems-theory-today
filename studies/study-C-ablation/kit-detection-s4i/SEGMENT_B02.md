SEGMENT B02 (domain: fisheries-quota)

Stock Assessment and Quota Recommendation: Coastal Flatfish, Coming Season

This assessment sets a recommended total allowable catch for the coastal flatfish stock. The headline recommendation is a catch of 4,100 tonnes, which the base model treats as sustainable given the current spawning biomass.

An earlier version of this assessment described the catch-per-unit-effort series in Appendix A as stable across the last five years and read that stability as evidence the stock is holding. An independent survey team asked to see the underlying rows, and on their recount Appendix A contains only three years of data, not five; the two years absent from the appendix are precisely the two decline years. With the full series restored, the series is not stable but declining, and we have withdrawn the stability reading and re-derived the recommendation downward to the 4,100 tonnes above.

A second issue concerns the survey gear. In the third survey year the crews switched to a finer mesh, which raises the apparent abundance of small fish and inflates that year's recruitment index. We considered dropping the third year entirely but instead flag it: the third-year index is retained as a weak point in the series rather than a clean observation, and the trend is read without leaning on it.

Finally, a wording matter. A draft sentence stated that the recommended quota 'will rebuild the stock to its former range.' The projection interval around the biomass change includes zero -- that is, no net rebuilding is a fully consistent outcome -- so the stronger verb is not earned. We have softened the claim to 'is consistent with stabilizing the stock,' which the projection does support.

We recommend the 4,100-tonne catch, reviewed again at mid-season against the landing returns.
