SEGMENT B10 (domain: wind-farm-noise-assessment)

Community Noise Assessment: Ridgeline Wind Installation

This assessment evaluates whether the ridgeline wind installation meets the community nighttime noise limit at the nearest homes. Sound levels were logged at three monitoring points over four weeks, and the assessment concludes that the installation meets the applicable nighttime limit at all three points, with one qualification and one correction noted below.

The nighttime limit is forty decibels measured at the residence. Our three monitoring points recorded four-week nighttime averages of 36, 37, and 39 decibels, all under the limit. The 39-decibel point sits close enough to the limit that we do not treat the margin there as comfortable, and we recommend a follow-up measurement at that home specifically.

One correction. A draft of this assessment reported the second point at 34 decibels, transcribed from the logger summary. The per-night detail table shows that value was the daytime figure; the nighttime average at the second point is 37 decibels, which is what we report here. The conclusion is unaffected -- 37 is still under the forty-decibel limit -- but the corrected figure is the one on record.

One qualification about method. The limit is written in terms of an average level, and an average can sit under a limit while brief, repeated peaks -- the blade-passing 'swish' that residents most often describe -- exceed it. This assessment measures the averaged level the standard names; it does not characterize the peak-to-peak amplitude of the swish, and a resident's complaint about that character is not something these averages can confirm or refute.

On the averaged nighttime level the standard specifies, the installation meets the limit at all three monitoring points.
