SEGMENT B08 (domain: freight-rail-inspection-backlog)

Freight Inspection Backlog: Staffing Review

This review evaluates the reported inspection backlog at the classification yard and whether it justifies adding a second inspection shift. The review concludes that a real backlog exists and that added inspection capacity is warranted, though the headline backlog figure overstates the gap and must be restated.

The reported backlog is 1,200 cars awaiting inspection. This headline count double-counts cars that failed a first inspection and were returned to the queue: each such car is entered once on arrival and again as a re-queued car, so one car can be tallied two or more times. The figure therefore overstates the number of distinct cars actually waiting.

It is worth being explicit about how large this effect is, because the same duplication has been described loosely in three places in the source report. Where the report says the yard is 'holding twelve hundred cars,' it is counting queue entries, not cars; a car re-queued after a failed check is one car, not two. And where the summary warns of 'twelve hundred distinct units awaiting inspection,' the word 'distinct' is simply wrong -- the tally has never been de-duplicated.

Netting out the re-queued duplicates leaves roughly 800 individual cars awaiting first inspection. On that corrected basis the backlog is smaller than the headline but still real, and the case for added inspection capacity stands. We recommend restating the backlog on a de-duplicated, distinct-car basis before the staffing decision is finalized.
