SEGMENT A07 (domain: elevator-safety-inspection-program)

Annual Elevator Safety Program — Recertification Summary

Program rule. Under the building's maintenance protocol, every passenger elevator must pass a full-load brake-and-governor test each year before it is recertified for service. The protocol states plainly: No passenger elevator is recertified until its full-load test for the year is completed and passed. The full-load test is distinct from the monthly visual inspection and cannot be substituted by it.

Scope this year. The building has fourteen passenger elevators, all of which came due for annual recertification in the reporting period. This summary records the recertification status of all fourteen.

Work performed. The monthly visual inspections were completed for all fourteen units and logged. The full-load tests were carried out on eleven units — cars 1 through 9, 12, and 14 — each of which passed. Cars 10, 11, and 13 were scheduled for full-load testing during the two weeks the load-test rig was out for repair, and the tests were not rescheduled before the reporting cutoff. No full-load result is recorded for cars 10, 11, or 13.

Certification status. The summary's closing line reads: All fourteen passenger elevators completed the required annual testing and are recertified for service.

Notes for next period. The load-test rig has been returned to service. A separate multi-year structural survey of the hoistway rails is planned for the building's twenty-year milestone and will be reported at that time.
