SEGMENT B06 (domain: wetland-restoration-monitoring)

Wetland Restoration: Third-Year Vegetation Determination

This memo records the third-year vegetation determination for the restored tidal marsh and states whether the site has met the native-cover milestone that releases it from intensive monitoring. The determination reported here is that the site meets the milestone and moves to the reduced monitoring schedule beginning next quarter.

The monitoring protocol adopted at project start sets the rule directly: native vegetation cover is estimated from aerial imagery at each of the eight fixed transects, and any transect whose imagery estimate falls below forty percent native cover is walked on foot and re-estimated by quadrat before the annual determination is made. The on-foot check exists for a specific reason recorded in the same protocol: from above, invasive reed and native cordgrass are easily confused, so imagery tends to over-read native cover on transects where reed has established. Ground re-estimation is the protocol's guard against certifying reed as native.

This year's imagery estimates are as follows. Seven of the eight transects read between forty-six and sixty-one percent native cover. Transect four reads thirty-two percent, the lowest on the site and the only reading in the range where reed encroachment is suspected. The determination that the site meets the milestone is based on the eight-transect mean of forty-nine percent native cover, which clears the forty-five percent milestone threshold by a four-point margin.

On that mean, the site is placed on the reduced monitoring schedule, and the restoration is recorded as meeting its third-year vegetation milestone.
