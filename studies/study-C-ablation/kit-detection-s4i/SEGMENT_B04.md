SEGMENT B04 (domain: pharmaceutical-supply)

Cold-Chain and Inventory Audit: Regional Distribution Warehouse

This audit reviews the regional warehouse's handling of temperature-sensitive stock over the past year and asks whether the site's cold-chain controls are sound enough to keep its accreditation. The audit concludes that the controls are sound with the corrections applied below, and recommends renewal.

The draft summary credited the site with 'zero temperature excursions all year,' citing the monitoring dashboard. The raw logger export tells a different story: eleven excursions above the eight-degree limit are recorded, all brief and all within the same refrigerated bay. The 'zero excursions' line was reading the dashboard's acknowledged-alarm count, not the excursion count; we correct it. Because every one of the eleven was investigated and the affected lots quarantined, the controls still pass, but the finding must rest on 'eleven excursions, all resolved,' not on a zero that did not happen.

The audit also proposed to rank the bays by risk using the number of alarms each generated, on the reasoning that more alarms means a less reliable bay. This has the relationship backwards: the busiest bay generates the most alarms because it is opened most often, not because it is least reliable. Ranking by alarms-per-opening removes the artifact, and on that basis the bay order changes.

A separate slip: the executive summary states that stock is reconciled 'weekly,' while the procedures section and every reconciliation record describe a monthly cycle. The records are internally consistent on monthly; the summary word is wrong and is corrected to monthly.

Finally, the draft claimed the site's controls 'ensure product quality.' Cold-chain monitoring measures temperature exposure; it does not measure potency or sterility, which are assessed elsewhere. We narrow the claim to what the monitoring can support: the site maintains the required temperature conditions. Accreditation renewal is recommended on that narrowed basis.
