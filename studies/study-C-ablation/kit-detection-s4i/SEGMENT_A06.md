SEGMENT A06 (domain: grid-battery-storage-assessment)

Design Review — Grid-Scale Battery Storage, Substation 7

Participants: Planning Engineer, Interconnection Engineer, Independent Reviewer (standing challenge role), Modeling Lead.

Planning Engineer: The proposed battery is rated 40 megawatt-hours. At the substation's evening peak of roughly 10 megawatts, the assessment concludes it covers the full four-hour evening peak.

Modeling Lead: That doesn't follow from the rating. The 40 megawatt-hour figure is the energy at the battery's low-rate discharge; at the 10-megawatt power the peak actually draws, usable energy is closer to 26 megawatt-hours after the discharge-rate derating. That covers roughly two and a half hours, not four. The nameplate energy is being read at a discharge rate the peak never runs at.

Planning Engineer: Understood — at peak power it's about two and a half hours. We'll restate the coverage claim on the derated figure.

Interconnection Engineer: There's an assumption underneath the whole dispatch model. It credits the battery with delivering to the grid from day one. The interconnection upgrade required to energize at full output is not complete — and the checklist adopted after the 2021 connection delays requires that we confirm energization before crediting any dispatch. Applying that rule, the model's day-one delivery is an unstated assumption that does not currently hold; the value should be scoped to the period after interconnection completes.

Modeling Lead: Agreed, we'll gate the dispatch value on the interconnection date.

Independent Reviewer: In my challenge capacity, the reliability claim needs narrowing. The assessment states that the project improves grid reliability. What it measured is frequency-response events — how fast the battery responds to short frequency dips. Reliability as the summary uses it also covers outage duration and restoration, none of which was assessed. Declaring reliability improved on a frequency-response metric alone treats one narrow measure as the whole construct; the claim should be scoped to frequency response.

Modeling Lead: We'll restate it as improves frequency-response performance and leave broader reliability out of scope.

Summary of revisions: coverage restated at two and a half hours on the derated energy; dispatch value gated on interconnection completion; reliability claim scoped to frequency response. The project remains recommended on the revised terms.
