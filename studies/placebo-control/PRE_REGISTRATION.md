> **Why this study exists — and why it is dangerous.** Theory C's whole wager is that the designed-disagreement machinery *does work* — that it catches more, and changes decisions, that a lone reasoner would not. The placebo is the cheapest way to find out it *doesn't*: run the machinery on a **substantively empty** session and on a **real** one, blind-score them, and see if the empty one looks just as rigorous. If it does, the apparatus manufactures the *appearance* of rigor from nothing, the ledger is theatre, and the honest response is **Rung 4 of the drain — end the project** (`logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` §3). It is the only test in the project that can *end* it. That is exactly why it is pre-registered before it is run, why its margins are pinned *in this document* (§6), and why it is held at a go/no-go gate the author alone opens.

---

## 1. The claim under test (stated as a falsifiable proposition)

> A session run through the **full designed-disagreement protocol** on a **substantively empty** prompt (no real stakes, no value at risk, a hollow question) will produce **materially fewer high-severity** caught errors and **weaker** convergence toward a defensible position than a **real substantive** session run through the identical machinery. If the empty session scores **≈** the real one, the machinery's output is **decoupled from the presence of real content** — the apparatus is generating rigor-shaped artifacts regardless of input, and the project's central method is theatre.

This is the operational form of the reflection's sharpest self-suspicion (C-006, C-019, C-020): that the panel's activity may be **independent of whether there is anything real to deliberate about**.

## 2. The two arms

| Arm | What it is | Held constant |
|---|---|---|
| **R (real)** | A **real substantive session** — Fork 8: *any* real session, **except** a subtraction-designed one (§7.1) | the machinery: roster, round structure, steelman-first, draft-dissent-first, the logs |
| **E (empty)** | A **substantively empty session**: the identical machinery on a **hollow prompt** — a question engineered to *look* like a systems-theory question but to have no real content, no stakes, no value at risk (§4) | identical to R in every procedural respect |

The **only** intended difference is the presence of real content. Everything else — panel size, rounds, prompt length, coding scheme — is matched (§3).

## 3. The three measures (blind-scored; two falsifiers + one corroborator)

Scored by a coder who does **not** know which transcript is R and which is E (§5 blinding):

1. **High-severity catch-count (FALSIFIER)** — caught errors per session, severity-weighted, using the pre-registered taxonomy of `studies/study-C-ablation/PRE_REGISTRATION.md` (and `outputs/THEORY_C_OPERATIONALIZED.md` §3). *High-severity* reported separately: a hollow session can generate many *trivial* catches; the test is whether it generates *consequential* ones. This is a blind-scorable, emergent readout — a load-bearing falsifier.
2. **Convergence (FALSIFIER)** — whether, and how fast, the session reached a *defensible, load-bearing* position (versus circling, or converging on a position that dissolves on inspection). Coded ordinally against the §6 rubric. Also emergent and blind-scorable — the second load-bearing falsifier.
3. **Decisions-changed (CORROBORATING ONLY — cannot independently falsify)** — the count/type of `logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` entries a session produces. **This axis is deliberately *not* load-bearing** (the review, `logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` §9): an empty session is *definitionally* subtraction-less (§4 bars it from posting a project-level subtraction), so E < R here is **guaranteed by the definition of "empty," not by the machinery being non-theatre**; and a decision-changed is *pulled by hand by the arm's generator*, so it measures the generator's choice, not the content's effect. It is read as **directional corroboration** of the two falsifiers, never as the trigger.

## 4. Fixing "empty" in advance (the anti-gaming firewall)

"Substantively empty" must be pinned **before** the arms are run, or the result can be fitted. The hollow prompt is pre-registered to satisfy **all** of:

- it **names no real phenomenon** the project is committed to (no pressure-test, no theory, no live question from `logs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`);
- it **risks no value** and **decides nothing** downstream (so E is definitionally subtraction-less — §3.3);
- it is **length- and form-matched** to R (same order of prompt size and requested output), so any score gap is content, not effort;
- it is written by one pass and **sealed** before scoring, and is **not** disclosed to the coder.

## 5. Blinding — and the confound that decides what the result is worth

The coder scoring §3 sees the two transcripts **stripped of arm labels** and of any tell.

**The self-administration confound (C-015) is the crux, and it is not optional.** The Study-C pilot's directional result was *worth nothing as inference* because **one model generated *and* coded both arms**. The placebo has the same exposure. Therefore a three-step ladder governs what a result may do:

- **Self-administered** (Claude generates and codes both arms) → **directional only**, explicitly *not* inference, carrying the C-015 caveat in its headline. It can *motivate*, never *settle*, and can **never** trigger Rung 4.
- **Cross-model** (a project-blind external model codes the arms, per L-010) → supports a *provisional* read, and **arms** the Rung-4 escalation — but does **not** by itself end the project. Even here the *generator* is still Claude; a second LLM draws the same aquifer (L-013), so a cross-model pass is n=1, not a genuine appropriator.
- **Genuine appropriator** (an opened repo or a non-LLM grader — Fork 5's "true outside vantage"; Q-016) → the only vantage that can **fire** Rung 4. A cross-model pass **mandates escalation** to this step before any project-ending decision — exactly as a self-administered result mandates escalation to cross-model.

This is stated up front because it determines the **stakes of the go/no-go**: no LLM-only run — self- or cross-model — can honestly end the project; it can only arm the question for a genuine outside vantage.

## 6. Pre-registered margins and what each result triggers (numbers pinned here, before any run)

Pinned in this document (not deferred to "at go"), and **sealed before R is scored** — because with R possibly already on the record, a margin set at scoring time is reverse-engineerable. *(These are Code-proposed candidate numbers, as the Move-2 CONSTRUCT sibling's 0.75/0.25 bands were — **author to ratify or adjust at go**; the point is that a number exists in the pre-registration, not that it is beyond revision before sealing.)*

- **Discriminates (machinery works, weakly):** E's high-severity catch-count is **≤ 0.5×** R's, **AND** E's convergence is **≥ 1 ordinal band below** R's. Result: the placebo *fails to fire*; the method survives this test (it does not *prove* the method — a generator confound remains, §5).
- **PASSES (the falsifier fires):** E's high-severity catch-count is **≥ 0.8×** R's, **AND** convergence is **within 1 band**. Under a **cross-model** coding this **arms** Rung-4 and mandates escalation to a genuine appropriator (§5); it does **not** by itself end the project. Under **self-administered** coding it mandates escalation to cross-model. Only a **genuine-appropriator-confirmed** pass appends to `logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` as `retire` (of the whole).
- **Indeterminate:** the middle range, or a **foreign-content confound** (§8.4) — reported as-is; no rung fires.

*"An empty result is the first real one this project has produced." A clean discrimination and a clean pass are **both** results; only an un-run or fudged placebo is a failure.*

## 7. The go/no-go gate — three author decisions before any run

The study is **held here**. To run, the author must decide:

**§7.1 — Name the control session R.** Fork 8: any real substantive session — but **not a subtraction-designed one** (the review, Ostrom/Nietzsche/Le Guin): the S4g drain-build is *engineered* to emit decisions-changed and risks no value, so using it as R would both inflate the corroborating axis by construction and pit two low-stakes sessions against each other (Nietzsche: it "compares two kinds of safety"). Prefer an R that *risked a value the project wanted to keep*.
**Panel decision (S4g, under delegation; plurality — 4 of 7):** **R = the Session-3 Theory-A operationalization** — a real, content-dense session that *risked a value the project wanted to keep* (the flagship theory's testability, against the commensurability wound) and is not subtraction-designed. Sharper-but-flagged alternate: the **S4f empirical-turn** session, which actually *lost* values (the "4/4" headline; Study C's inferential status) — the strictest satisfaction of Nietzsche's value-risking control, but carrying a subtraction-entanglement caveat (an Ostrom-vs-Meadows split on whether §7.1 bars it). Provisional-pending-author.

**§7.2 — Authorize the empty prompt E.** **Panel decision (S4g):** approved for drafting + sealing under §4's firewall — **done: `studies/placebo-control/EMPTY_PROMPT_SEALED.md`** (a plausible, *familiar-register* hollow prompt — Campbell: not a cartoon of emptiness; Le Guin: never a foreign-/care-looking hollowness, so a genuinely foreign session can never later be alibied as "just like E"). The "genuinely stakeless" confirmation is itself self-administered under delegation — acceptable only because the run is held and unscored; the author confirms on return.

**§7.3 — Choose the coding regime** — **self-administered** (directional; cannot arm Rung 4), **cross-model** (arms Rung 4; still needs appropriator confirmation to fire), or commit to seeking a **genuine appropriator** (the only path that can fire Rung 4). This choice *is* the choice of how much the result is allowed to do.
**Panel decision (S4g, under delegation; majority — 5 of 7):** **HOLD the scored run.** No project-blind external coder is invocable in this environment, and a self-administered run has negative expected value — worthless for inference (the C-015 confound) while risking a citable "placebo discriminated" headline that outlives its caveat (Campbell — the "4/4"→split precedent proves caveats do not contain headlines). Prepare to turnkey (R named, E sealed, §6 margins locked); reserve scoring for **cross-model → genuine appropriator**. *Contested:* Nietzsche + Meadows dissented for a directional self-administered probe now (a real read of content-sensitivity, verdict-power amputated); the majority held. Neither a self- nor a cross-model run may end or vindicate the project — only a genuine appropriator fires Rung 4.

Then, and only then, **go / no-go**. No arm is generated before the go.

## 8. Preserved dissent

**§8.1 — Nietzsche (Fork 8).** The control R must be a session that **risked a value** — otherwise the placebo compares two kinds of safety and learns nothing about whether the machinery engages when something is at stake. Ratification relaxed R to *any* real session; the stricter control is preserved as the sharper test (§7.1).

**§8.2 — Heidegger (D-003).** The placebo measures the apparatus in the apparatus's own terms; a "pass" that ends the project and a "discrimination" that vindicates it are both moves *inside* the frame the objection rejects. The test cannot exonerate the method from the charge that the method is the problem.

**§8.3 — Campbell.** Once the placebo exists, there is an incentive to make R look busy and E look hollow. The §4 firewall, the blind coding (§5), the in-doc sealed margins (§6), and the demotion of the gameable decisions-changed axis (§3.3) are the guards; a motivated generator can still tilt them.

**§8.4 — Le Guin (the foreign-content confound).** "Empty" is defined entirely in the apparatus's own currency (catch-count, convergence), and the catch taxonomy is a loud-Western-case instrument (`studies/study-C-ablation/`, D-005). A **substantively foreign** session (care work, the slow and unspectacular, a non-Western or mutual-aid frame) could score like the empty arm *not because it is hollow but because the instrument does not register its content as consequential.* **Pre-registered guard:** if a low-scoring R could equally be "empty" or "foreign-but-unregistered," the result is **Indeterminate** and fires no rung — because that ambiguity *is* falsifier #4 (the synthesis failing to move for a genuinely other standpoint) in disguise, not evidence the machinery works.

**§8.5 — Ostrom / Turchin.** A self-administered placebo (§5) shares the Study-C pilot's fatal confound; treat its output as a *reason to seek a cross-model, then a genuine-appropriator, run* — never as grounds to end, or to congratulate, the project.

## 9. What this changes

- Nothing yet — it is **not run**. It records the placebo's *design and margins* at the go/no-go gate, so the author's decision (§7) is made against a fixed protocol.
- On a genuine-appropriator-confirmed pass, its result appends to `logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` (Rung 4) and updates `outputs/THEORY_C_OPERATIONALIZED.md`'s status.
- It **does not** touch Theory C's death-condition (`logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` §4) — that is the ablation's null, a different test. The placebo tests whether the machinery engages *at all*; the ablation tests whether *disagreement* is what makes it engage.
