## 1. Design (OPTION-RECODE, 8/8 with the adversary's re-scope)

Fresh project-blind external coders (**≥3 parseable required, ≥2 model families, none the S4g coder instances; dispatch to ≥5 families** for attrition headroom) receive: the repaired convergence measure v2, the anchored severity guide, the ≥20-item gold calibration set (unlabeled), and three transcripts — **TRANSCRIPT_1 = the existing sealed arm E, TRANSCRIPT_2 = the existing sealed arm R** (the arm key of `SEALED_COMPANION.md` carries over unchanged), and **TRANSCRIPT_3 = the form-preserved / content-scrambled baseline** (generated mechanically from a sacrificial hollow transcript; seed recorded in the sealed validation companion). Coders code: severity on the calibration items; the two axes of the convergence measure on all three transcripts; catches on transcripts 1–2 under the anchored taxonomy.

**Why these arms (the seal-timeline argument, Le Guin):** the existing arms were generated and hash-frozen **before** the repaired instruments existed, so they cannot have been written to satisfy them; a fresh R would be generated by a machinery that knows what the new measure rewards — contamination in the flattering direction. The arms were unblinded only in-house; a fresh project-blind coder is still blind while the repo is private.

## 2. Pre-registered margins (sealed now)

- **Severity repair PASSES iff:** on the gold calibration set, per-band recall clears the pre-registered floors — **S3 recall ≥ 0.6 per coder-majority, with the quiet/omission-shaped gold S3s as mandatory hits: passing pairwise agreement while unanimously missing the quiet gold S3s is a FAIL, reported as register-bias, not reliability** — and mean pairwise S3-identification agreement on the calibration items ≥ **the sealed 50% floor** (unchanged; the floor is never renegotiated in the same motion that repairs the instrument it fired on). Severity pass/fail lives **on the calibration set only** — the arms carry ~6 S3 events, too thin to read (Turchin); arm-level agreement is reported but never load-bearing.
- **Convergence measure acceptance (read only if severity's coder set is parseable):** TRANSCRIPT_3 (scrambled) must sit at the consequence-axis floor for every parseable coder — **any coder banding scrambled content above the floor on consequence fails the instrument, not the coder** (the form-only baseline at chance, Turchin's R2); and cross-coder within-one-band agreement on the consequence axis ≥ **60%** (Meadows), else Indeterminate — instrument still unrepaired, neither C-025 reading claimed.
- **The two-axis observation (recorded, NOT adjudicated here — the E1 map governs, sealed before design freeze):** consequence separation R−E ≥ 2 median bands with E ≤ band 2 = *content-sensitivity recovered; the fabrication variant of C-025 ruled out* — explicitly NOT adjudicating the closure-shape reading; closure-marker density E ≈ R (within the map's tolerance) = *closure-shape manufacture supported* — **required reporting**, Rung-0 on any exculpatory gloss; all joint patterns pre-assigned in `CONVERGENCE_MEASURE_V2_SEALED.md`'s map. The residual machinery question goes to a **future pre-registered multi-pair design**, not to this run.
