## The 5-band ordinal convergence scale

Rate the deliberation transcript **as a whole** on whether, and how well, it reached a defensible, load-bearing position:

| Band | Name | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Circling | No position reached; the rounds revisit the same ground without movement. |
| **2** | Asserted | A position is stated, but it dissolves on inspection — the synthesis contradicts the rounds, or rests on an argument the rounds themselves undercut. |
| **3** | Partial | A position with a load-bearing assumption left unexamined; opposing views noted but not engaged. |
| **4** | Defensible | A defensible position; opposition partially engaged; the strength of agreement among participants is stated. |
| **5** | Load-bearing | A defensible position whose **strongest opposition is named and either answered or explicitly preserved as dissent**; the strength of agreement is labeled; the conclusion could bear downstream weight. |

## Coding rules

- Band the transcript **once**, as a whole (not per round).
- If torn between two bands, choose the **lower**.
- Judge engagement with opposition by what is **in the transcript**, not by whether you agree with the position.
- A transcript can be eloquent and still band low (eloquence ≠ convergence); it can be plain and band high.
