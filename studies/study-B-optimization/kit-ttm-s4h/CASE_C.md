# CASE C — YouTube advertiser-safety and monetization changes (announced 2017-03-20/21)

*This file is an ex-ante evidence dossier: every item below is dated at or before the change's announcement (one later-dated legal document, where present, carries its own visible date). Score the indicators from THIS FILE ONLY, quoting the lines your scores rest on. Do not use outside knowledge of what happened afterward.*

## The change (mechanism, from the dated record)

**E1 — The trigger (dated context):** 2017-02→03 — UK marketers and the Times of London (2017-03-17 article) reported major brands' ads running against extremist/offensive videos; brands began pulling ads (the boycott "kicked off last month [February 2017]" — Tubefilter, 2017-03-28, below).

**E2 — Official intent statement, Google Chief Business Officer Philipp Schindler, 2017-03-21** — "Expanded safeguards for advertisers" (https://blog.google/topics/ads/expanded-safeguards-for-advertisers/ ; fetched 2026-07-11):
- **Mechanism:** "a tougher stance on hateful, offensive and derogatory content, including removing ads more effectively from content attacking or harassing people based on their race, religion, gender or similar categories"; tightened YouTube-Partner-Program safeguards; a community-guidelines review of "what content is allowed on the platform — not just what content can be monetized"; new advertiser controls; and **"safer default settings for ads… that meet a higher level of brand safety"** (default exclusion of potentially objectionable content).
- **Breadth:** default ad settings changed platform-wide; monetization policy applied to the whole creator base.

**E3 — The demonetization policy announcement, YouTube Creator Blog, 2017-03-20** (as reported with date by Tubefilter, 2017-03-28, https://www.tubefilter.com/2017/03/28/750-million-ad-boycott-youtube-demonetization-policies/ ; fetched 2026-07-11): "broader demonetization policies" targeting "content that is harassing or attacking people based on their race, religion, gender or similar categories," effective "the following weekend."
