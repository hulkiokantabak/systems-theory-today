## The change (mechanism, from the platform's own ex-ante record)

**E1 — Official announcement, YouTube Creators blog, 2012-03-09** — "Changes to Related and Recommended Videos" (originally youtube-creators.googleblog.com, now https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/changes-to-related-and-recommended ; fetched 2026-07-11):
- **Mechanism:** Related and Recommended videos shift from click-based serving to **time-watched/engagement-based** serving — "time watched is one of the best indicators of a viewer's engagement"; "clicks aren't always the best way to predict whether you'll be interested in a video."
- **Breadth:** "Related and Recommended videos… to the right of the player and on the homepage" — the core discovery surfaces, all users; no limited rollout stated.
- **Pre-decision projection (the platform's own, in the announcement itself):** *"videos that don't [keep people engaged] will not be surfaced as prominently, **which will impact a video's viewcounts over time**."* — an explicit, dated, ex-ante acknowledgment that the change would cost the **view count**, the unit to which creator monetization and (via per-play pre-roll impressions) ad delivery were then tied.

**E2 — TechCrunch, 2012-10-12** — "YouTube Changes Its Search Ranking Algorithm To Focus On Engagement, Not Just Clicks" (https://techcrunch.com/2012/10/12/youtube-changes-its-search-ranking-algorithm-to-focus-on-engagement-not-just-clicks/ ; fetched 2026-07-11):
- The same metric shift extended to **search ranking** ("reward engaging videos that keep viewers watching"); YouTube anticipated "the time viewers spent watching videos from search will increase"; concurrent addition of watch-time metrics to Analytics; the piece notes the suggested-videos change preceded it — a **family of changes, one direction, spanning 2012**.

## The divergence this dossier documents (for the later, blind, ex-ante coding pass)

- **Selection/engagement pressure:** FOR the change — watch-time is the deeper engagement signal; the recommendation engine "wanted" it.
- **Short-term revenue pressure:** AGAINST — the monetizable unit (view-initiated ad impressions; creator payouts) was, by the platform's **own ex-ante statement (E1)**, going to fall.
- **Honest complication (recorded, not resolved):** the platform's public rationale framed watch-time as **long-term** revenue-aligned. Whether "short-term revenue vs selection" satisfies the slot's divergence criterion is exactly what the blind coding pass must adjudicate — it is **not** adjudicated here.
