## The change (mechanism, from the dated public record)

**E1 — Douyin/ByteDance announcement, 2021-09-18** (reported contemporaneously: The Register, "Tick, tick, tick … TikTok China just limited kids to 40 minutes' use each day," 2021-09-20, https://www.theregister.com/2021/09/20/douyin_youth_mode_time_limits/ ; fetched 2026-07-11):
- **Mechanism:** all users who have authenticated with their real names and are **under 14** are moved into **"youth mode"**: usage capped at **forty minutes a day**, no access **between 10pm and 6am**, and the mode serves **"wholesome, curated content"** — i.e., for the affected cohort, the engagement-ranked recommendation feed is replaced by a curated pool with hard time limits.
- **Breadth:** mandatory (not opt-in) for the real-name-verified under-14 cohort; the cohort is a bounded slice of the user base (the dossier makes no claim about its share of impressions — the coder judges breadth from the mechanism facts).
- **Stated rationale (the platform's own):** "At the same time, we will work harder to provide high-quality content so that teenagers can learn knowledge and see the world on Douyin."
- **Pre-decision projection:** no platform-issued projection of an engagement or revenue cost appears in the gathered ex-ante record (I3: no documented pre-decision projection; not to be back-filled).

**E2 — The regulatory context (dated legal documents):**
- The Register (E1 above) states contemporaneously that "restricting the amount of time that kids can spend in Douyin is **required by Chinese law**" — the change is a compliance act under state mandate, not a voluntary product decision.
- **CAC (+MIIT/MPS/SAMR), "Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions"** — released **2022-01-04**, effective **2022-03-01** (English translation: DigiChina/Stanford, https://digichina.stanford.edu/work/translation-internet-information-service-algorithmic-recommendation-management-provisions-effective-march-1-2022/ ; fetched 2026-07-11, S4g): mandates anti-addiction protections for minors and bars "exploitative or addictive recommendations." *Post-dates the September 2021 change (date visible above); included as the codification of the same regulatory pressure line, per the S4g leads-file fetch.*

## What this dossier documents (for the blind, ex-ante coding pass)

- A **state-mandated de-optimization**: the direction of regulatory compulsion and the direction of the engagement/selection pressure point **opposite** for the affected cohort — the divergence class the widened pool exists to test, from outside the Western ad-platform ecology.
- **Honest limitations (recorded, not resolved):** the primary corporate record is Chinese-language and reached here through dated English reporting and a translated legal text — a thinner documentary layer than the Western cases' first-party blogs (itself a D-005 instrument effect, recorded in `LEADS_NONWESTERN_CARE.md`); and the affected cohort is a bounded minor-user slice, which the coder must weigh in breadth, not have weighed for them here.
