Status: **LEADS FILE (Session 4g) — names candidate case-families and the evidence classes a future sourcing pass would fetch. Contains NO factual claims about any case: nothing here is sourced, dated, or dossier-grade; treating any line below as evidence would be the recall-dressed-as-citation failure this project bars (L-013).** Companion to `EX_ANTE_DOSSIER_YT2012.md` / `OUTCOME_DOSSIER_YT2012.md`, per the ratified widen-before-grade order and D-005 (whose crises are on the list).

> **Why this file exists (Le Guin, from the S4g design panel — travels as binding dissent):** "the live-sourcing route runs through English-language trade press, earnings calls, and SEC-shaped evidence classes — and the named candidate is a third loud Western ad-platform case. Widening that widens the *n*, not the *standpoint*. If the loop ships the near-control dossiers but not even a leads file for WeChat/Douyin and a quiet care/mutual-aid system, then 'widen-before-grade' has quietly become 'widen-within-the-West-before-grade.'" This file is the first payment on that debt — a debt, not a decoration.

## Family 1 — Non-Western platform cases (Q-015; D-005)

- **WeChat (Tencent)** — candidate slot: does TTM→pressure hold outside the Western ad-funded model, where the revenue engine (payments, mini-programs, gaming cross-promotion) is not primarily feed-advertising? Evidence classes a sourcing pass would fetch: Tencent annual/interim reports and earnings transcripts (HKEX filings, English editions); documented Moments-feed or mini-program ranking changes with dated official statements; Chinese regulatory records (CAC directives on recommendation algorithms, 2021–22 algorithm-registry filings) as *exogenous* de-optimization events.
- **Douyin (ByteDance)** — candidate slot: the state-mandated youth-mode/time-limit changes — a *forced* de-optimization where the selection pressure and the mandate point opposite. Evidence classes: dated CAC/state-media directives; ByteDance official announcements; documented engagement effects in reputable reporting.
  - **First dated primary, FETCHED (S4g big loop, 2026-07-11):** the CAC (+MIIT/MPS/SAMR) **"Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions"** — released **2022-01-04**, effective **2022-03-01**; full English translation: DigiChina/Stanford (Creemers/Toner/Webster rev. Creemers), https://digichina.stanford.edu/work/translation-internet-information-service-algorithmic-recommendation-management-provisions-effective-march-1-2022/ ; alternative translation: https://www.chinalawtranslate.com/en/algorithms/ . Mandates anti-addiction protections for minors and bars "exploitative or addictive recommendations" — a **regulator-forced de-optimization event class** with a dated legal text, exactly the exogenous-pressure divergence the slot family needs. *This upgrades the Douyin lead from a name to a document; case-level dossiers (which platform change, when, what outcome) remain to be sourced.*
- **Honest note:** both cases' primary records are partly Chinese-language and partly non-public; a sourcing pass must either reach dated primary documents (translated official texts, filings) or report the classes empty — never paraphrase from recall.

## Family 2 — Quiet / care / mutual-aid systems (the construct's scope test)

- **A crisis-line or peer-support platform** (e.g. a text-based crisis service) — candidate slot: a system whose core objective is *not* an engagement metric; if TTM is undefined there, that is the scope finding the construct lock anticipates (`CONSTRUCT_THREAT_TO_METRIC.md` §7: "the construct is parochial to attention-market platforms").
- **A mutual-aid coordination platform** (neighborhood-scale, volunteer-run) — same scope test from the cooperative side; evidence classes: governance documents, funder reports, any documented ranking/matching change and its stated rationale.
- **Honest note:** these systems' records are thin, private, or ethnographic rather than filings-shaped — which is itself D-005's point: the instrument's evidence classes were built for loud systems. A future pass may need a *different instrument*, not just different cases.

## What this file is not

Not a dossier; not evidence; not a claim that any named system had any particular change or effect. It commits the project to *look here next*, with the same fetch-and-date discipline the YT2012 dossiers used — or to report the emptiness honestly.
