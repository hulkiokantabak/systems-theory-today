# CASE E — YouTube recommendation/search re-ranking to watch-time (announced 2012-03-09 / 2012-10-12): WHAT HAPPENED AFTERWARD

*You are coding the OUTCOME of this change. Below: the stated goal and the pressure lines as of the announcement (facts of the public record, no scores), then the dated evidence of what happened afterward. Judge only from this file.*

**Stated goal (public, at announcement): surface videos viewers actually watch — treat time-watched as the better engagement signal than clicks.**

**Pressure lines: DIVERGENT — the engagement-selection pressure favored the watch-time switch; the short-term revenue pressure ran AGAINST it (the platform itself projected view counts, then the monetizable unit, would fall).**

## The dated outcome record

**O1 — Official note, YouTube Creators blog, 2012-05-23** — "A note about recent changes" (https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/note-about-recent-changes ; fetched 2026-07-11):
- Responds to creator complaints of **view and subscriber drops** after the March change: "if people are clicking on your videos, but not sticking around to watch, your videos won't get shown as often in suggested and recommended videos and growth in new views may slow."
- **Held course:** no walk-back; creators directed to "create compelling videos that people stick around for." No monetization concession; a "$200 million to promote our partners' content" investment cited.

**O2 — Business Insider (via Yahoo Finance), 2015-07-03, Jillian D'Onfro** — "The 'terrifying' moment in 2012 when YouTube changed its entire philosophy" (https://finance.yahoo.com/news/terrifying-moment-2012-youtube-changed-120123634.html ; fetched 2026-07-11):
- **Immediate effect:** "In one fell swoop, the site's daily view count got completely demolished. We're talking a plunge of **about 20% in one day**."
- **Creator revenue distress:** Cristos Goodrow (then director of engineering, search & discovery) recalls creators calling — "I think something's broken on YouTube"; one: "I'm gonna have to go back to work! I'm not going to be able to pay my mortgage anymore!"
- **Internal register:** Goodrow — "You can imagine how controversial and terrifying such a thing was!" *(Gray-zone item: a retrospective account touching pre-decision internal expectations; per the construct's I3 discipline it is **low-power** for ex-ante coding and is filed here, on the outcome side, not in the ex-ante dossier.)*
- **Trajectory:** "the analytics proved that just as many people were visiting the site as always and that watch time was indeed increasing significantly. By May, the average watch time had swelled to four minutes, from just one minute the year before." No reversal through the article's date (2015).

**O3 — Persistence within the record fetched:** the metric family was *extended*, not reversed, within 2012 itself — search ranking moved to time-watched in October , seven months after the view plunge was visible.
