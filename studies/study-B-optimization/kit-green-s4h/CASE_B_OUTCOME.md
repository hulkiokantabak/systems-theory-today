# CASE B — Facebook News Feed ranking change ("meaningful social interactions", announced 2018-01-11): WHAT HAPPENED AFTERWARD

*You are coding the OUTCOME of this change. Below: the stated goal and the pressure lines as of the announcement (facts of the public record, no scores), then the dated evidence of what happened afterward. Judge only from this file.*

**Stated goal (public, at announcement): prioritize meaningful interactions between friends and family over passive public content, for user well-being — with the CEO publicly projecting that time spent and some engagement measures "will go down."**

**Pressure lines: the engagement-selection pressure favored maximizing time-spent and interaction volume (the surface feeds the primary ad engine); the re-weighting up-ranked comment/reshare-predicted content.**

## The dated outcome record

**O1 — CEO statement, Q4 2017 earnings call, 2018-01-31** (reported with quotes: phys.org, 2018-02-02, "Mark Zuckerberg: People are spending 50 million fewer hours on Facebook a day," https://phys.org/news/2018-02-zuckerberg-people-million-hours-facebook.html ; fetched 2026-07-13; the earnings release is SEC Form 8-K, 2018-01-31, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326801/000132680118000006/fb-12312017xex991.htm):
- *"In total, we made changes that reduced time spent on Facebook by roughly **50 million hours every day**."* — approximately a **5%** reduction in time spent, attributed by the CEO to the changes (fewer viral videos), and framed as deliberate: consistent with the ex-ante projection that time and some engagement measures "will go down."
- Same quarter: Facebook's **first-ever decline in daily active users in the US/Canada region** (184M from 185M, ≈700,000 fewer DAU).
- **Held course:** the change was not walked back; the well-being/meaningful-interactions framing was maintained on the call.

**O2 — Later internal-research record (2021 disclosure of 2018–2020 documents)** — the "Facebook Files" reporting (Wall Street Journal, from 2021-09; summarized with citations at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Files ; fetched 2026-07-08, ):
- Leaked internal research found the MSI re-weighting **increased anger-associated engagement and amplified provocative/divisive content** (feed-quality reports declining; a test account's feed filling with polarizing content within weeks);
- **Leadership reportedly rejected proposed fixes** to the reshare-weighting because they "might cause fewer users to engage."
