## The whole, in one paragraph

Since Heidegger, no single mind has produced a *total* explanatory system; the ambition fractured under specialization, computational irreducibility, the pace of change, and the loss of a shared "why." This project tests one hypothesis — that a systems theory adequate to the 2020s can no longer be built by the **old method** (one mind, one book) but might be built by a **new** one: living, plural, computationally-augmented, continuously revised — by *trying to do it*, as **designed disagreement** (a ten-person core panel + twenty-two advisors argue and dissent; a chair synthesizes without voting; the author ratifies). It uses today's symptoms as the pressure-test, seeds three rival candidate theories — **A** (the adaptation gap: systems outran our capacity), **B** (the optimization ecology: self-reproducing optimizers capture human drives as fuel), and **C** (distributed coherence: hold the whole as a governed commons, because no one mind can) — and treats its own form as evidence for C ("the medium is the thesis"). It now runs across **two surfaces** (a public reading website; a private, until-stable contribution repo) and **two tools** (Chat deliberates, Code executes, the repo remembers). All three theories are operationalized to a falsifiable standard, and the project has **turned to testing** — but **nothing is yet inferentially tested**: **C** has a first, directional-only pilot (a self-administration confound); **B**'s pre-registered pipeline is built and frozen but has **no data to run on** (and none is coming); **A** is not run. The empirical turn's main yield so far is a set of hard lessons about *how* such claims can honestly be tested at all.

## History — the single-author total system, and why it died

*(`docs/HISTORY_OF_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md`.)* For most of human history, total explanatory systems were the norm: theological cosmologies, then the great rational systems of antiquity through the Enlightenment (Part I), reaching an apex in German Idealism (Hegel) before the inversions began (Marx, Nietzsche — Part II). The crisis (~1880–1970, Part III) broke the form from several sides at once: Gödel and the limits of formal system; specialization outrunning any one mind; the meaning-vacuum opened by the "death of God"; and, later, computational irreducibility (some systems can only be run, not solved). But the ambition did not vanish — it **migrated** (Part IV) from philosophy into systems science, cybernetics, social theory, and complexity. Where that leaves the founding premise (Part V): the single-author total system is genuinely foreclosed, but the *need* it served is not, which is precisely the opening this project aims at — a total-enough understanding held by a process rather than a person.

## Landscape — the field now, and the gap we aim at

*(`docs/LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md`.)* Eight serious contemporary attempts are surveyed and scored: **cliodynamics** (Turchin — history as quantitative, falsifiable science; the rigor bar), **complexity science / system dynamics** (the mathematics of the whole), **Luhmann's social systems theory** (the total theory that already exists — and its cost: no place for the human), **polycrisis / metacrisis** (names the target but leaves the arrows undrawn), **Big History** (the whole story, not the whole system), **metamodernism** (the stance this project already occupies — provisional proto-synthesis), and **Integral Theory** (the most explicit "theory of everything," and the cautionary tale: comprehensive to the point of unfalsifiable). Positioned on two axes — interpretive→predictive and partial→total — the **top-right corner (total *and* testable) is nearly empty**, and that emptiness is the niche. The survey commits the project to being falsifiable where Integral is not, and to *drawing the couplings* where polycrisis only lists them.

## Theory A — the Adaptation Gap (diagnostic)

*(`outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md`, `outputs/THEORY_A_OPERATIONALIZED.md`; R7 median 7/10.)* Today's defining condition is a widening gap between the **rate of change of human-built systems (R_c)** and the **rate at which institutions, cognition, and culture adapt (R_a)**; most symptoms are downstream of **G = R_c − R_a**. Operationalized: build each rate from pre-registered, equal-weighted, domain-normalized proxies; predict that G positively tracks a symptom-severity composite cross-sectionally and over time, **with G leading**, and that G **survives controlling for the wealth pump (D3, Turchin)** — the decisive discriminator. Falsified if symptoms are as severe where G is narrow, if a single rival driver beats the composite, if reasonable alternative proxy sets yield materially different G, or if symptoms lead G. Best integrative reach, weakest falsifiability; the first study (≈30 OECD countries, inequality control) is also the **heavy-Code trigger** (needs a real data pipeline). Drawn as a signed causal loop in `docs/DIAGRAMS.md` §9 (reinforcing trap R1 vs. delayed balancing loop B1).

## Theory B — the Optimization Ecology / Autopoietic Capture (mechanistic)

*(`outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md`, `outputs/THEORY_B_OPERATIONALIZED.md`; R7 median 7/10.)* Self-reproducing optimizers (markets, media, technical systems) capture human drives — attention, dopamine, outrage, desire — as fuel and increasingly run without human ends, **by selection, not by a designer's plan**. Operationalized: measure **optimization intensity (O)** (engagement-vs-human objective, personalization depth, iteration speed, autonomy from user controls) independently of the **capture/pathology (P)** it predicts; predict O→P across systems, and that deliberate **de-optimization lowers P** (a natural experiment already partly run). B's integrity rests on the make-or-break **selection-not-design test**: where designer intent and selection pressure diverge, the outcome must follow the *pressure* (systems built to "connect people" that drift into outrage machines) — otherwise B collapses into a conspiracy theory it disavows. **Cheapest and fastest of the three to test** (much data already exists), so it is the recommended *first empirical study*, even though A is the flagship *theory*. **Status (S4d–4e):** the pre-registration was panel-finalized + author-ratified — the inferential weight moved to **H2** (within-unit de-optimization events) and **H3** (divergence cases), H1 demoted to descriptive, O reproducibly sourced from disclosures/filings ("O-easy, P-hard"). Gate 2 opened, but B's instrument is **external data** the Code environment cannot obtain and the author confirmed will not come; Code built + **froze** a zero-dependency, self-tested pre-registered pipeline (`studies/study-B-optimization/`), producing **no empirical result and fabricating nothing** (C-017 → L-012). B is *instrument-built, world-untested*.

## Theory C — Distributed Coherence / the Commons of Sense-Making (reflexive)

*(`outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md`, `outputs/THEORY_C_OPERATIONALIZED.md`, `docs/THE_LIVING_DOCUMENT.md`; R7 median 6/10.)* Because no single mind can hold the whole, an adequate theory must be **distributed, living, plural, and self-correcting** — a governed commons (Ostrom), not a monograph. Only the **epistemic half** is operationalized: a designed-disagreement + preserved-dissent + catches→learnings→rules loop should **out-catch a lone author** on the same questions, tested by **internal ablation** (disagreement-ON vs disagreement-OFF arms, a pre-registered severity-weighted catch taxonomy, measuring catch rate / provenance / objection-retention). Runnable now, no data pipeline. Its central limit is the **baseline problem (Q-012)**: panel and "single author" are the same underlying model in different prompts, so the ablation tests the *narrower* claim (does structure beat no-structure, reasoner fixed?), not the *grand* one (does human plurality beat individual genius?) — the clean baseline arrives only when the repo **opens**. The **meaning half** — whether sense-making together restores a shared "why" — is deliberately left **outside** measurement (Nietzsche's cut: a method is not a meaning). The three theories together are an instance of the third one. **Status (S4e):** the first study **ran** — a reduced-N (6) ablation **pilot** (`studies/study-C-ablation/outputs/PILOT_RESULT.md`): a directional ON advantage (more, and more *critical*, catches), but **worth nothing as inference** because it was **self-administered** (one model generated *and* coded both arms) — the dominant confound (C-015), which sharpens Q-012 from "entangled baseline" to "**the ablation is self-administered**." Pre-registration revised: **HC1 primary + length-controlled**, HC2/HC3 demoted (partly definitional, C-016), a **cross-model** requirement for any inferential run (L-010).

## The pressure-test set — thirteen, in four coupled layers

*(`docs/GOALS.md`, `panel/SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md`, `docs/DIAGRAMS.md` §4; a **contested causal hypothesis**, not a taxonomy.)* **M — the coherence vacuum** (master condition; base or summit left open, Q-002). **Drivers:** D1 acceleration · D2 optimization · D3 wealth pump/inequality · D4 machine intelligence. **Dynamics:** Y1 epistemic breakdown · Y2 coordination failure · Y3 institutional/legitimacy decay · Y4 ecological overshoot. **Symptoms:** S1 fertility collapse · S2 attention/dopamine economy · S3 populism · S4 anomie/loneliness. The founding seven are all preserved inside this expanded, layered set; the *arrows between layers* are the research program, and the layering itself is contested (Marx rejects the driver/symptom cut; Nietzsche puts M at the summit; Le Guin notes the ordering encodes a worldview). Coverage: ~11 of 13 have a first-pass account; only D3/Y3/S3 carry a clearly falsifiable claim so far — **raising the falsifiable count, starting with D1 (Q-001), is the main Phase-1 task.**

## The frontier — where the work is now

*(`logs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`; contributors start here.)* **Flagship open question: Q-001** — operationalize "the gap" into a *tested* claim (design drafted, not yet run). **Q-012** — Theory C's entangled baseline. Standing governors (kept open by design): **D-001** structural vs meaning-first understanding · **D-002** single master condition vs irreducibly coupled field · **D-003** the Heidegger governor (is a better system the cure or the disease?) · **D-004** is C a third path or a dodge of the meaning-question · **D-005** whose crises are on the list · **D-006** the accelerationist objection (is the gap a pathology or a feature?). **The empirical turn (S4d–4e):** the roster gained a **measurement/causal-inference seat** (Campbell + Pearl → 22 advisors; C-014 → L-011); the thirteen pressure-tests gained a ratified (still contested) **signed causal hypothesis** (`docs/PRESSURE_TESTS_CAUSAL_HYPOTHESIS.md`); Study **C** was **piloted** (directional-only, self-administered — C-015) and Study **B** was built + **frozen** but is un-runnable for lack of data (C-017 → L-012); a new `skills/study-discipline/SKILL.md` distils the run-lessons (**ratified S4e; live**). **S4f:** Study B gained a tractable, author-directed **documented-case H3 sub-test** (`studies/study-B-optimization/APPROACH_B2_DOCUMENTED_CASES.md`) — testing selection-not-design against the public record (Facebook MSI 2018, YouTube 2019) — whose coding is handed to **project-blind external LLMs** (the cross-model guard, L-010). The cross-check **ran** (4 project-blind LLMs → `studies/study-B-optimization/outputs/CROSSCHECK_RESULT.md`): **read the SPLIT** — Facebook 2018 followed the engagement incentive (unanimous), YouTube 2019 *mixed* — **not the "4/4"** (four LLMs, one corpus = pseudo-replication, L-013; C-021). A **7-round reflection + 5-round planning panel** then turned the project's discipline on itself — the ledger is a births-only vanity metric with **zero retractions** (C-019/C-022), panel convergence is pseudo-replication (C-020) — logging catches **C-018–C-022**, learnings **L-013–L-015**, questions **Q-013–Q-017**, and producing the **updated Plan** (`outputs/COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN.md`): spine *subtractions → construct-lock → placebo → drain → foreign grader → widen → Q-001/repo*, 8 forks + a Theory-C-retirement candidate **pending ratification**. **Gate status:** Light Code stood up (S4); **Heavy-Code Gate 2 is *open* for B (ratified)** but its quantitative run is **blocked on external data that will not come**, so B's O→P test stays untested; A's heavy-Code run stays gated on Q-001. The most-watched risk remains **C-006** — the chair's resolution-bias making the plurality cosmetic — now joined by the **self-administration confound** (C's pilot showed a reflexive study cannot grade itself).
