# OPEN QUESTIONS & LIVE DISAGREEMENTS

Version: 0.3 · Status: Living · Last updated: Session 4g

*A standing record of what the project has **not** settled — every genuine open question and every live disagreement, logged as it surfaces. The author asked for this explicitly, and the reason is sound: in a project built on designed disagreement, the unresolved questions and the standing splits are the most revealing part of the record. They are where the real thinking is, they are what a contributor should attack first, and they are the honesty check against the chair's drift toward premature closure (L-001). Nothing here should be quietly resolved by a later session's tidy-up; a question leaves this log only when it is genuinely answered, and the answer is recorded with its date and its dissent.*

**Two registers.** **Open Questions (Q)** — things we don't yet know and could, in principle, come to know (empirical, methodological, or design questions with a possible answer). **Live Disagreements (D)** — standing splits where reasonable panelists hold opposed positions on grounds unlikely to be settled by evidence alone; several are *designed* to stay open as governors.

**Status values:** `open` (unanswered, answerable) · `standing` (a governor — kept deliberately unresolved) · `tracked` (parked, to revisit) · `answered` (with date + how).

## OPEN QUESTIONS

### Q-001 — Is Theory A falsifiable, and how do we operationalize "the gap"?
Theory A (the Adaptation Gap) claims pathologies flow from the rate of systemic change outrunning adaptive capacity. But a gap between two rates is only a theory if both rates can be *measured*. Can we define **rate-of-systemic-change** and **rate-of-institutional/cognitive-adaptation** as indices from real data, such that the gap is computable and its correlation with the pressure-test symptoms is testable? Until we can, Theory A risks being an elegant restatement of "things are changing fast," which explains nothing (Turchin's bar; Goal S2). **This is the single highest-value Phase-1 task.**
*Raised:* S1 R7, restated S2. *Status:* `open` — the flagship task; **a full operationalization design now exists in draft** (`outputs/THEORY_A_OPERATIONALIZED.md`, S3): two rate-indices with candidate proxies, a normalization discipline, a cross-sectional + longitudinal prediction, crisp falsification conditions, the discriminator against Turchin's wealth pump, and a pre-registered first study. The design is specified but **not yet run/tested**, so Q-001 stays open — the bar is a *tested* claim, not a stated one. This is also the concrete trigger for heavy Code (data pipelines). **Advanced (S4g, panel-ratified admission; provisional-pending-author):** a full designed-disagreement deliberation on the commensurability core (run as the placebo's real arm, `studies/placebo-control/arms/ARM_R_TRANSCRIPT.md`; unsealed after scoring) reached a **majority** position — *R_c and R_a are not naturally commensurable, but commensurability can be constructed and **tested** rather than assumed* — under four amendments: matched-pair time-ratios as the preferred primitive; the family of domain gaps as the primary object (scalar G a declared summary); pre-registered robustness tripwires + convergent/discriminant validation *before* outcomes, with a corruption clause; and ordinal-unless-earned interpretation. The "manufactured commensurability" charge is thereby converted into one of Theory A's own falsifiers. Dissent preserved verbatim: Heidegger (full no), Michell (unproved quantitativity — summoned, non-voting), Nietzsche (partial — commensuration is valuation; sign the scale), Le Guin (the scalar's gravity). *Lives in:* `outputs/THEORY_A_OPERATIONALIZED.md`, `outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md` (A), `outputs/COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN.md` (Phase 1), `DIAGRAMS.md` §9.

### Q-002 — Is the coherence vacuum (M) the base or the summit?
Is the loss of shared meaning the quiet *ground* beneath the machinery (material drivers produce it), or the loudest *fact* of which everything else is an echo (it produces the rest)? The Session-2 layering placed drivers "below" and meaning at "Layer 0," which a layout can make look like an answer — but it is precisely the Round-5 split, unresolved. We have deliberately drawn M as *coupled to all layers without asserted primacy.*
*Raised:* S1 R5, sharpened S2 R3. *Status:* `standing` (kept open by design; see D-001). *Lives in:* `panel/SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md` R3, `DIAGRAMS.md` §4.

### Q-003 — What is the *unit* of adaptation?
Theory A needs to say *what* is failing to adapt fast enough. Institutions? Individual cognition? Culture and shared narrative? Law? Probably all of them, at different rates — which means "the gap" may be a *family* of gaps with different time-constants, not one number. Naming and separating these units is a prerequisite for Q-001.
*Raised:* S2 (implied by R3 layering). *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md` (A).

### Q-004 — How does Theory C avoid the Integral trap?
Theory C proposes a living commons that integrates many perspectives. Integral Theory (Wilber's AQAL) also integrates all perspectives — and is widely judged unfalsifiable, a taxonomy so accommodating nothing can contradict it (`LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md` §7). What structural feature keeps Theory C from becoming the same comprehensive-but-empty map? Candidate answer: C must *host falsifiable sub-theories and preserve their defeats*, not merely file every view as "partly right." But this needs to be built into the method, not asserted.
*Raised:* S2 (from landscape survey). *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md` (C), `LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md` §7.

### Q-005 — Is bracketing meaning a limit or a virtue?
The project deliberately does **not** claim to deliver "lived shared meaning" (the standing meaning-governor). Is that honest incompleteness a permanent virtue — the thing that keeps us from becoming Integral Theory or a religion-substitute — or a limit we should eventually try to overcome? The meaning-bloc (D-001) says a systems theory that brackets meaning is not a theory of a *human* world; the structural bloc says faking the meaning column is exactly how frameworks go soft. Both may be right at once.
*Raised:* S1 R5, sharpened by `LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md` §9. *Status:* `standing`. *Lives in:* `LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md` §9, `GLOSSARY.md` ("understanding").

### Q-006 — Where is the line between comprehensive and unfalsifiable?
Session 2 added five pressure-tests; the same instinct, unchecked, could add fifty. Meadows: a model with too many variables predicts nothing. Le Guin: restraint is also a worldview, and parsimony can be a polite name for a blind spot. So: what is the *criterion* for admitting a variable? Session 2's working answer — "it must replace a vague symptom with a measurable driver, or be cut" — is a start, but the line is not yet principled.
*Raised:* S2 R2. *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `panel/SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md` R2; the disagreement form is D under this same tension.

### Q-007 — Is the form genuinely an argument, or a rationalization?
The project claims "the medium is the thesis" — that a living commons *demonstrates* Theory C. But does building the thing you're arguing for count as evidence *for* it, or does it beg the question? A working repository proves the method is *possible*, not that it is *right* or that it *reaches the whole*. We should be able to state what would show the reflexive wager to have **failed** (e.g., the commons never forms; or it forms but produces only consensus mush, no falsifiable theory).
*Raised:* S1 R6 (Nietzsche: a method is not a meaning). *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `README.md` (reflexive wager), `docs/METHOD.md`; disagreement form at D-004.

### Q-008 — Is AI a driver, or a new master condition that reorders the board?
Session 2 admitted machine intelligence as **D4**, one driver among four. But a strong case says AI is not a peer variable — it changes what "adaptation" (D1), "coordination" (Y2), and "epistemics" (Y1) *mean*, and may itself become the fastest-moving thing the gap is measured against. Should D4 be promoted to sit beside M as a second organizing pole? Deferred, but flagged as potentially structure-altering.
*Raised:* S2 R1–R2. *Status:* `tracked` (revisit when AI's role in each layer is mapped). *Lives in:* `panel/SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md`.

### Q-009 — At what stage does the project go to Code — and should it ever?
Two kinds of "Code": **light** (a real git repo + static-site build + deploy) and **heavy** (executable models, data pipelines, computational sense-making). When does each become worth the executor's cost, and is heavy Code ever warranted, or is the project's value as a thinking-and-writing artifact?
**Refined reading (Session 3, author-ratified):** the trigger for *light* Code is **capacity, not preference** — deliberation is best in Chat, and there is no virtue in leaving early; *while Chat holds the working context, stay in Chat.* But the context ceiling has been reached (a compaction and run-outs), so **stand up Light Code now for durable memory and keep the center of gravity in Chat**, running deliberation *over* the shuttle and drawing registers (§5) instead of the whole archive. Two surfaces: a **public reading website** now, a **private contribution repo** until the skeleton is stable, then opened for forking (`THE_LIVING_DOCUMENT.md` §7). **Heavy Code** stays gated on Q-001 — whose operationalized claim now exists **in draft** (`outputs/THEORY_A_OPERATIONALIZED.md`) but is **not yet tested**, so the gate is *approaching, not passed*. The "maybe never" branch stays honest.
*Raised:* S2 (author's direct question). *Status:* `open` (light-Code trigger met; heavy-Code approaching). *Lives in:* `docs/CHAT_CODE_WORKFLOW.md`, `DIAGRAMS.md` §7–§8, `outputs/COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN.md`.

### Q-010 — How thin can the slice be without starving deliberation? → RESOLVED (multi-register)
**Resolved (Session 3, author): don't choose a thickness — hold several registers at once.** A session loads the pointer (R0: map + last digest), the frontier (R1: open questions), and a Code-maintained **gist of the whole** (R3) *always*; adds its working documents at full resolution (R2); and pulls full deep text (R4) only on demand. The document stays whole *in view* without any worker holding it whole *in context*; the thin/thick binary dissolves. Full model in `docs/CHAT_CODE_WORKFLOW.md` §5; generalized as **L-008** (optionality over a forced single choice).
*Raised:* S2. *Status:* `answered` (S3). *Lives in:* `docs/CHAT_CODE_WORKFLOW.md` §5.

### Q-011 — How is a Chat/Code divergence reconciled?
The repo is the shared memory, but Chat's *intended* change and Code's *on-disk* state can drift (a work-order describes an edit that no longer matches the file; two cycles touch the same document). What is the reconciliation rule when they disagree? The commitment: never silently overwrite one with the other — surface the divergence and resolve it deliberately (the author's own branching-and-reconciliation discipline, applied to the shuttle). The concrete triggers and resolution procedure remain to be specified.
*Raised:* S2. *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `docs/CHAT_CODE_WORKFLOW.md` §9.

### Q-012 — Can Theory C's reflexive claim be tested without an entangled baseline?
C's operationalization (`outputs/THEORY_C_OPERATIONALIZED.md`) measures whether the commons *out-catches a lone author* — but in the project's current form the "panel" and the "single author" are the **same underlying model in different prompts**. So the ablation tests the *narrower* claim (does a disagreement-**structure** beat an unstructured process, reasoner held fixed?), not the *grand* one (does distributed **human** plurality beat individual genius?). The narrower result is still worth having; the uncontaminated baseline only arrives when the repo **opens** and real external forks appear (which ties the *testing* of C to the two-surfaces plan, not just its building). What interim baselines are legitimate, and how much can the ablation honestly claim before then?
*Raised:* S4. *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `outputs/THEORY_C_OPERATIONALIZED.md` §4, `docs/CHAT_CODE_WORKFLOW.md` §8.

### Q-013 — Is Theory B's H3 conditional ("selection beats stated intent iff the change threatens the engagement metric") correct?
The Study-B cross-check (4 project-blind LLMs) read Case A (a broad engagement re-weighting) as outcome-follows-incentive and Case B (a narrow, engagement-neutral de-optimization) as *mixed*, converging on a **conditional**: selection wins where the change conflicts with the core metric, relaxes where it is engagement-neutral. Is that conditional real, or an artifact of two cases on a shared corpus?
*Raised:* S4f (cross-check). *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `studies/study-B-optimization/outputs/CROSSCHECK_RESULT.md`.

### Q-014 — What CONSTRUCT (not target) should be pre-registered for the cross-check, and what number would settle "selection beats intent"?
The panel split: pre-register the **classifier** for "threatens the metric" before grading (Turchin) vs the objection that a pre-registered target is still a target, so pre-register the **construct** and its validity first (Campbell). Until resolved, H3's "threat to the metric" risks being read off the very outcome it predicts (circularity).
*Raised:* S4f (reflection). *Status:* **`answered` (S4g)** — resolved (author-ratified) to **pre-register the held-out CONSTRUCT + its validity first** (planning-panel majority + Campbell), Heidegger's refusal preserved; a **numeric kill-threshold** is fixed. Enacted in `studies/study-B-optimization/CONSTRUCT_THREAT_TO_METRIC.md` — TTM, four ex-ante indicators, a 0.75 / 0.25 band cut, an anti-circularity two-dossier firewall, out-of-sample confirmation. The construct is **locked but not yet tested** (the confirmatory pool is unsourced — the widening move). *Lives in:* `studies/study-B-optimization/CONSTRUCT_THREAT_TO_METRIC.md`, `skills/study-discipline/SKILL.md`.

### Q-015 — Does the selection-not-design claim survive a non-Western / care / quiet-system case pool?
All cross-check cases were large Western, corporate, *loud* platforms (D-005 applied to the check). Does H3 hold for WeChat, a mutual-aid or care system, or a case where selection pressure and revenue point **opposite** (the near-control the study lacks)?
*Raised:* S4f (reflection; D-005). *Status:* `open`. *Lives in:* `studies/study-B-optimization/APPROACH_B2_DOCUMENTED_CASES.md`.

### Q-016 — Would a genuinely foreign, blind grader name any outcome the plurality actually changed?
The project's central reflexive bet (Theory C) is that designed disagreement out-catches a single sharp mind. Run alone, would a lone adversary catch everything the panel caught? Would a **foreign** (non-LLM, non-Claude) grader find the plurality changed any decision? Until then designed disagreement's construct validity is ~0 (L-013/L-015).
*Raised:* S4f (reflection). *Status:* `open` (ties to the placebo test + the foreign-grader move). *Lives in:* `logs/REFLECTIONS.md` (S4f).

### Q-017 — Does the metabolism need a subtraction / retraction operator?
The learning loop has **zero retractions** — it only accumulates (C-022). Should it gain a way to **retire a theory, un-count a catch, strike a governor that never bit, or track decisions-changed** instead of births? The ontological camp objects that operationalizing a governor is how it dies; the rigor camp says "name the number or strike the governor."
*Raised:* S4f (reflection). *Status:* **`answered` as a *governance* decision (S4g); the *ontological* question stays live as D-003.** The author ratified (Fork 2/3/4/6) **installing the drain** — a *decided* subtraction operator (retire / retract / downgrade / strike) with an append-only **decisions-changed log**, an Ostrom-*inspired* severity-ordered ladder (not a clock), a pre-written Theory-C death-condition (retire on a null, Turchin — *armed, not firing*: it needs a genuine appropriator, not an LLM-only null), and a first strike **held** (the never-bit audit found no clean over-claim candidate — awaits the author naming one; Fork 4). Enacted in `logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` (seeded with three prior Move-1 subtractions, DC-001…003; the operator itself has fired zero times), distilled into the **updated L-014** (no new learning-ID minted — the drain's own principle) and a proposed **Ground Rule 24**. What is answered is only the *governance* choice (should the operator exist as a matter of how we run); whether the operator *should* exist at all — Heidegger's charge that installing it is the enframing (D-003) — is **not** answered and cannot be by a ratification vote; the operator is barred (by a standing, self-enforced rule) from ever striking that objection (`logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md` §5, §7). *Lives in:* `logs/DECISIONS_CHANGED.md`, `logs/REFLECTIONS.md` (S4f), `docs/METRICS.md`, `docs/GROUND_RULES.md` (R24).

## LIVE DISAGREEMENTS

### D-001 — Structural understanding vs meaning-first understanding
**The split:** Is *understanding* the grasp of structure and mechanism (with meaning a separate, later project) — or does an understanding that brackets meaning fail to be understanding of a *human* world at all?
**Sides:** *Structural bloc* — Aristotle, Kant, Meadows, Turchin, Luhmann (build the mechanism; meaning is a different question). *Meaning bloc* — Nietzsche, Heidegger, Le Guin, Arendt (a theory that explains everything and means nothing has explained nothing that matters).
**Why it's revealing:** it is the project's deepest fault line; it decides what "success" even means and shadows every later choice (including how diagrams are drawn — see Q-002).
*Status:* `standing`. *Origin:* S1 R5.

### D-002 — A single master condition vs an irreducibly coupled field
**The split:** Is there one upstream cause (the adaptation gap? the death of shared value?) from which the rest flow — or is the condition an irreducible field of mutually-causing factors with no root to find?
**Sides:** *Single-root* — the plurality that endorsed Theory A as "most upstream"; Nietzsche (one wound: the death of values). *No-root* — Turchin and Marx (a master condition trades testability for a satisfying story; it is a coupled system, not a root).
**Why it's revealing:** it determines whether the project seeks *the* mechanism or *a map of couplings*; it is why the deliverable is three rival theories, not one.
*Status:* `standing`. *Origin:* S1 R3, reaffirmed S2 R3.

### D-003 — Is a better system the cure or the disease? (the Heidegger governor)
**The split:** Is building a more adequate systems theory the way through the crisis — or is the very impulse to grasp everything as an optimizable system the fullest expression of the crisis itself?
**Sides:** *The project's premise* (a better joining helps) vs *Heidegger* (the enframing thinking that treats world-as-system is what must be questioned, not perfected), with *Le Guin* partly aligned (the control-impulse is the problem).
**Why it's revealing:** it is the objection that, if right, invalidates the whole enterprise — which is exactly why it is seated permanently and re-raised whenever the project congratulates itself (C-002).
*Status:* `standing` (governor — never to be resolved, only kept live). *Origin:* S1 R1/R6.

### D-004 — Theory C: a genuine third path, or a dodge of the meaning-question?
**The split:** Does a living, plural, procedural commons genuinely offer a way to "think the whole" that the monograph could not — or is it a sophisticated evasion, substituting *process* for the *meaning* the old totalities supplied?
**Sides:** *The reflexive wager* (a procedural totality is the honest post-monograph form) vs *Nietzsche and Heidegger* (a method is not a meaning; a well-run process that means nothing is still nihilism, better organized).
**Why it's revealing:** it is the test of the project's central hope; Q-007 is its answerable sub-question (what would count as the wager failing?).
*Status:* `standing`. *Origin:* S1 R6.

### D-005 — Whose crises are on the list?
**The split:** Is the pressure-test list a reasonable selection of the age's defining problems — or does the selection (and the omissions) encode the list-makers' standpoint, leaving out what they don't feel (care work, the global South's crises, the non-human, the slow and unspectacular)?
**Sides:** *The working list* (these are defensible, high-salience systemic problems) vs *Le Guin* (a list of crises is already a worldview; parsimony can be a blind spot with good manners — D and Q-006 are the same tension seen from two sides).
**Why it's revealing:** it keeps the project honest about its own vantage — the very thing its founding premise says no single standpoint can transcend. The correction is not to pretend to a view from nowhere but to *name the vantage and invite its correction* (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
*Status:* `standing`. *Origin:* S2 R1–R2.

### D-006 — Is the adaptation gap a pathology or a feature? (the accelerationist objection)
**The split:** Theory A treats the gap between change and adaptation as the thing to *close*. Accelerationists and techno-optimists hold the opposite: acceleration is the engine of progress, and the answer is to *speed adaptation up* (build faster institutions, augment cognition) or to *let the old forms break* — not to slow the change.
**Sides:** *Theory A* (the gap is the pathology; many symptoms are downstream of it) vs *the accelerationist position* (the gap is a transitional feature; resisting acceleration is the real error).
**Why it's revealing:** it is the strongest external rival to our flagship theory, and taking it seriously is what separates Theory A from a reactionary lament about "things moving too fast."
*Status:* `open`/`standing` (a genuine rivalry to adjudicate, not just preserve). *Origin:* `LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md` §8.

## How this log is used

- **Contributors start here.** The fastest way to add value is to attack an open `Q` (especially Q-001) or to take a side in a `D` with new argument or evidence (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- **The chair is audited here.** If a later session's synthesis appears to have "resolved" a `standing` item, that is a catch — check whether the resolution is real or the L-001 drift recurring.
- **Answers are earned, and dated.** When a `Q` moves to `answered`, the entry keeps its history: the question, the answer, the date, and any preserved dissent. We do not delete the question; we record that it was met.
