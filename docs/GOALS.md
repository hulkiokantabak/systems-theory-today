# GOALS

Version: 0.2 · Status: Draft (author to ratify) · Last updated: Session 2

*What we are trying to do, stated so plainly that we can later tell whether we did it.*

## The primary goal

Build a **systems theory adequate to the world of the 2020s** — a framework that can account, in a single coherent (if provisional and plural) order, for how today's coupled systems produce today's condition — using **new methods**, because the old method (one mind, one book) can no longer reach.

We treat this as a genuine open question, not a foregone conclusion. It is possible the honest finding is that no such theory can be built, or that only fragments can. That finding, rigorously reached, would itself be a result worth publishing.

## The pressure-tests

A candidate theory earns credibility to the degree it illuminates these, without special pleading and without collapsing them into a single glib cause — *and*, since Session 2, to the degree it draws the **arrows between them**: how a driver produces a dynamic produces a symptom, and how symptoms feed back.

**The founding seven** (honored; the project's origin): fertility, the dopamine/attention economy, the insufficiency of "post-truth," the meta-question of *why now*, climate, coordination failure, and the resurgence of populism/authoritarianism — beneath them a master puzzle: the loss of shared belief and coherent understanding.

**The expanded set** (Session 2, `panel/SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md`). The panel re-debated the seven, admitted five more, and — over live dissent — restructured the flat list into **four coupled layers plus a master condition**, presented as a *contested causal hypothesis*, not a taxonomy. All seven are preserved; their new homes are noted. Additions marked **[+]**.

- **M — The coherence vacuum** *(master condition; whether it is the base or the summit is left open — Q-002).* The loss of shared meaning, belief, and a common frame for understanding. *(Absorbs old #4, "why now," as the same demand from the inside.)*

- **Layer 1 — Drivers** *(deep, slow, generative)*
  - **D1 Acceleration** — change outrunning adaptation (the adaptation gap; reframes old #4).
  - **D2 Optimization dynamics** — self-reproducing optimizers capturing human drives.
  - **D3 Wealth pump / inequality [+]** — structural wealth concentration and elite overproduction.
  - **D4 Machine intelligence [+]** — computation reshaping labor, cognition, epistemics, coordination.
- **Layer 2 — Dynamics** *(couplings the drivers produce)*
  - **Y1 Epistemic breakdown** (old #3) · **Y2 Coordination failure** (old #6) · **Y3 Institutional & legitimacy decay [+]** · **Y4 Ecological overshoot** (old #5, generalized beyond climate).
- **Layer 3 — Symptoms** *(what we feel)*
  - **S1 Fertility collapse** (old #1) · **S2 Attention/dopamine economy** (old #2) · **S3 Political form: populism** (old #7) · **S4 Anomie / the loneliness epidemic [+]** — the felt face of M.

*Tracked as effects/amplifiers, not top-level tests (revisit): migration, financialization (folded into D3), pandemics/biosecurity, geopolitical/war risk, energy transition (folded into D4/Y4).*

The layering is itself contested — Marx rejects the driver/symptom cut, Nietzsche puts M at the summit not the base, Le Guin notes the ordering encodes a worldview (all preserved in `SESSION_2_PRESSURE_TESTS.md` and `logs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`). The visual form is the concept map in `docs/DIAGRAMS.md` §4.

## Secondary goals

- **Answer the historical question honestly** (done in draft: `HISTORY_OF_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md`): what total systems existed, who built them, when they fell, and what is being tried now.
- **Situate ourselves in the living field honestly** (done in draft: `LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md`): survey the serious contemporary attempts — cliodynamics, complexity science, Luhmann, polycrisis/metacrisis, Big History, metamodernism, integral theory, and more — say what each reaches and can't, and locate the precise gap we aim at. Public-facing (site + reports), because a theory that ignores its neighbors is not adequate to today.
- **Assemble and operate a panel** of real thinkers as designed disagreement (`panel/`).
- **Produce our own candidate theories** — at least two, ideally three — as living seeds others can extend or break (`outputs/CANDIDATE_THEORIES.md`).
- **Define our terms** — "understanding," "today," and "systems theory" are contested and must be argued, not assumed (`docs/GLOSSARY.md`).
- **Build toward code** — migrate the candidate theories from prose to machine-readable models to runnable simulations (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §6).
- **Make it public and forkable** — GitHub repository + website, so others contribute, contradict, and extend (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- **Make it learn** — a catches-and-learnings loop so the project corrects itself over time (`logs/`).

## What success looks like (and how we'll know)

Because "we built a total theory of everything" is not a checkable claim, we define success operationally:

- **S1 — Illumination.** For each pressure-test (now thirteen, in four layers), the project offers a systems-level account — *and draws the couplings between them* — that a thoughtful skeptic finds *more* explanatory than the loose talk currently on offer (measured against the best of the field in `docs/LANDSCAPE_OF_CONTEMPORARY_SYSTEMS_THEORIES.md`, not its memes). Measured by red-team review, not self-assessment.
- **S2 — Falsifiability.** At least one candidate theory generates a claim that could, in principle, be shown wrong — a prediction, a mechanism, or a model output. (Turchin's standard: a theory that risks nothing explains nothing.)
- **S3 — Coherence with honesty.** The candidate theories hang together *and* the project openly records where they don't, where they were overturned, and what remains unexplained.
- **S4 — Livability.** The repository is genuinely usable by a stranger: a contributor can find where to add, fork, and propose a rival without breaking the structure.
- **S5 — Reflexivity that pays off.** The method itself (designed disagreement + synthesis + continuous revision) demonstrably catches errors an individual author would have missed — logged in `logs/`.

## Non-goals (to keep us honest)

- Not a claim to finality. Every output is provisional; "proto-synthesis," in the metamodern phrase.
- Not neutrality-as-emptiness. The panel takes sides; the project has views. (See `GROUND_RULES.md`.)
- Not prophecy. We explain and model; we do not predict a fixed future. Where we forecast, we forecast *conditionally* and to be acted upon.
- Not a single doctrine imposed by the chair. The chair synthesizes and does not vote; the author ratifies; contributors dissent.

## The reflexive goal (the one that ties form to content)

Demonstrate, by building it, that a systems theory for today can be **held as a living commons** rather than authored as a monograph — that the way past the impossibility of the old method is a *procedural* totality: many minds, in structured disagreement, continuously integrated. If the project works, it is itself a small instance of its own third candidate theory.
